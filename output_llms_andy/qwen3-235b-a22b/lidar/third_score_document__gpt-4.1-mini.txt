  According to the API, `ChRigidTerrain` uses `AddPatch()` to add terrain patches with material and size, then `Initialize()` is called without parameters. The code uses a different pattern, which is not consistent with the documented vehicle example.  
  **Deduct 15 points** for missing the proper terrain patch creation and initialization steps.

  **Deduct 15 points** for incorrect use of `chrono.ChFramed` (likely a typo or misunderstanding).

- **Variable Definitions**:
