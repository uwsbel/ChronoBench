Typically, gravity would be set to Earth's gravity or another meaningful vector.   The visualization is set up using `irrlicht.ChIrrApp`, with lights and camera added, and assets bound and updated. This is consistent with PyChrono visualization best practices.  
  **No deduction** here.

- **Simulation Loop:**  
  The loop applies gravitational forces between particles manually, then steps the simulation and renders the scene. This is a correct approach for custom force application.  
  **No deduction** here.
