  `vehicle.Initialize()` is called after setting parameters, which is correct.

  `vehicle.GetSystem().SetCollisionSystemType(chrono.ChCollisionSystem.Type_BULLET)` is called after initialization. According to best practices, collision system type should be set before initialization to ensure proper setup. Setting it after initialization may not have the intended effect.

  Uses `chrono.ChContactMaterialNSC()` with friction and restitution set. This is correct.

- **Driver Gains:**  
  Gains are set with proportional terms only, which is acceptable for a simple controller.

- **Render Step Calculation:**  
  `render_steps = math.ceil(render_step_size / step_size)` is correct.

- **Simulation Loop:**  
  Synchronize and Advance calls are in correct order.

- **Minor issues:**
