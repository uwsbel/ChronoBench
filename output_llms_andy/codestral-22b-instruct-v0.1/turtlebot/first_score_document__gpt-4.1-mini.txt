  - The code calls both `app.DoStep()` and `system.DoStepDynamics(0.01)` in the loop. According to the API, `app.DoStep()` internally calls `system.DoStepDynamics()`. Calling both is redundant and may cause the simulation to advance twice per loop iteration, which is incorrect.

**Deduction:**  
- Calling both `app.DoStep()` and `system.DoStepDynamics(0.01)` causes the simulation to advance twice per loop, leading to incorrect simulation behavior. Deduct **15 points**.
