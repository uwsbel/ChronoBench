- **Joint Creation:**

- **Visualization Setup:**  
  - Uses `chronoirr.ChIrrApp()`, which is not in the documented API. The documentation specifies `chronoirr.ChVisualSystemIrrlicht()` and its methods.

  - Visualization setup is incomplete and inconsistent with the documented API.  

- **Simulation Loop:**  
  - Uses `application.Begin()`, `application.GetDevice().Run()`, `application.Render()`, and `application.End()`. These are not documented methods; the documented visualization loop uses `vis.Run()`, `vis.BeginScene()`, `vis.Render()`, and `vis.EndScene()`.

  - No proper scene begin/end calls for rendering.
