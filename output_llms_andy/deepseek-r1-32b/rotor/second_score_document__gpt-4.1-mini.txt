According to the API, joints are initialized between two bodies, or two bodies and a frame. Initializing a joint directly between a node (which is part of the FEA mesh) and a rigid body may not be correct. Typically, for FEA nodes, special constraints or attachments are used. This could cause logical errors or runtime errors.
