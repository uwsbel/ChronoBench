### 2. Correctness (30 points)

- **Use of `SetSectionRotation`:**  
  The code uses `msection.SetSectionRotation(45 * chrono.CH_RAD_TO_DEG)`. According to the API, `SetSectionRotation` expects radians, but `CH_RAD_TO_DEG` is a conversion factor from radians to degrees (≈57.2958). Multiplying 45 by `CH_RAD_TO_DEG` converts 45 radians to degrees, which is incorrect. The correct usage should be either `45 * chrono.CH_DEG_TO_RAD` (to convert degrees to radians) or just `chrono.CH_C_PI / 4` (for 45 degrees in radians).  
  This is a **significant error** that will affect the beam section orientation.  
  **Deduct 15 points.**
