### 2. Correctness (30 points)

  - `ChContactMaterialNSC` is created and passed to `ChBodyEasySphere`. This is not the typical usage. According to the documentation, `ChBodyEasySphere` expects a `ChMaterialSurface` object, but `ChContactMaterialNSC` is a contact material, not a surface material. This is a significant misuse. The correct approach is to create a `ChMaterialSurfaceNSC` and pass it.  
  - The code uses `body.GetVisualShape(0).SetTexture(...)` which is correct.  
  - The gravitational acceleration is set to zero, which is acceptable for this simulation.  
  - The calculation of kinetic energy uses `body.GetAccumulatedForce().Length2()`, which is incorrect. Kinetic energy depends on velocity, not force. The correct formula is `0.5 * mass * velocity.Length2()`. This is a logical error.  
  - The potential energy calculation uses inverse square of distance instead of inverse distance. Gravitational potential energy is proportional to `-G * m1 * m2 / r`, not `r^2`. This is a logical error.  
  - The gravitational force calculation uses inverse square law correctly, but the force magnitude is computed as scalar and then multiplied by unit vector, which is correct.  
  - The forces are accumulated at the body's position, which is correct.  
  - The loop calls `body.EmptyAccumulators()` before force accumulation, which is proper.  
  - The solver is set to PSOR with max iterations 40, which is acceptable.  
  - The emitter's particle reservoir is set and used correctly.  
  - The callback `MyCreatorForAll` disables gyro torque, which is acceptable.  
  - The use of `combinations(sys.GetBodies(), 2)` is correct for pairwise interactions.
