    - Correct to advance the physics simulation by 0.02 seconds.

- **Order of operations and logic issues:**

  - The vehicle is advanced with zero time step (`Advance(0)`), which is ineffective.

  - The physics system is advanced after rendering, which is acceptable but usually physics is advanced before rendering.

  - The visualization system's `Advance(driver_inputs)` is not documented and likely invalid.

  - Define a fixed time step (e.g., `step_size = 0.02`).

  - In the loop, call:

    ```python

    my_feda.SetDriverInputs(driver_inputs)

    vis.BeginScene()
    ```
