### 6. **Simulation Loop & Dynamics**

```python
while vis.Run():
    time = my_feda.GetSystem().GetChTime()
    driver_inputs = driver.GetInputs()
    my_feda.Advance(time - my_feda.GetSystem().GetChTime())

    vis.Render()
    my_feda.GetSystem().DoStepDynamics(0.02)
```
**Evaluation:**  
- The loop structure is generally correct.
- **Issue:**  
  - The `Advance()` call on `my_feda` uses `time - my_feda.GetSystem().GetChTime()`, which is problematic because `time` is obtained from `my_feda.GetSystem()`, so `time - my_feda.GetSystem().GetChTime()` will be zero or very small, leading to no advancement.
  - The correct approach is to call `my_feda.Advance()` with a fixed timestep (e.g., 0.02), or to manage time properly.
- **Suggestion:**  
  Use a fixed timestep for `Advance()`, e.g., `my_feda.Advance(0.02)`.

- The `DoStepDynamics(0.02)` is called after `Advance()`, which is acceptable but redundant if `Advance()` already advances the system.

**Overall:** The loop structure is acceptable but could be improved for clarity and correctness.
