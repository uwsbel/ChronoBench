  ```python

  ```

- According to the API, the two `ChVector3d` arguments specify the attachment points **in the local coordinate systems of the two bodies**. Using `(0,0,0)` means the spring attaches at the center of each body.

- This inconsistency will cause the spring to be attached at different points than expected, possibly causing strange dynamics or visual artifacts.

- **Recommendation:** Use `(0,0,0)` for both points unless there is a specific reason to offset.
