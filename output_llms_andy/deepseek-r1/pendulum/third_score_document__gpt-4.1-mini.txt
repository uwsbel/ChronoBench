The joints are placed at `joint_pos` and `joint_pos + (2,0,0)`. This means pendulum 1 is offset by 1 unit from the joint, and pendulum 2 is offset by 2 units from the joint. This is inconsistent with the pendulum length `L=2`. Normally, the pendulum 1 should be positioned so that its joint is at one end of the pendulum, and pendulum 2 should be positioned relative to pendulum 1's end.

This means the joint between ground and pendulum 1 is at `(0,0,1)`, but pendulum 1 is at `(1,0,1)`. The joint frame is not coincident with the pendulum 1 position, which may cause unexpected behavior.

  - This suggests the pendulum bodies are not positioned correctly relative to their joints, which can cause incorrect simulation behavior.

**Deduction:** Logical error in body positioning relative to joints: **-10 points**
