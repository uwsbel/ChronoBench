  - The line `vehicle.GetSystem().SetCollisionSystemType(chrono.ChCollisionSystem.Type_BULLET)` is called **after** vehicle initialization. According to best practices, collision system type should be set **before** initialization to ensure proper collision system setup. The typical pattern is to modify inputs **before** calling `driver.Synchronize()`. Here, inputs are modified after `driver.GetInputs()` but before `driver.Synchronize()`, so this is acceptable.

  - The `render_frame` variable is incremented but never used.  
  - The `step_number` is incremented every loop but not reset or bounded; this is acceptable but could be improved.
