### 2. Correctness (30 points)

- **Custom Force Implementation:**

  - The `ComputeForce` method signature is correct.

  - The force calculation has a critical error in the damping force calculation:

    ```python
    damping_force = -self.damping_coef * (rel_vel ^ rel_pos).Length()
    ```

    The cross product magnitude is used for damping, which is physically incorrect. Damping in a spring-damper system is proportional to the relative velocity component along the spring direction, i.e., the dot product of relative velocity and normalized relative position vector.

    Correct damping force magnitude should be:

    ```python
    damping_force = -self.damping_coef * rel_vel.Dot(rel_pos.GetNormalized())
    ```

    Using the cross product magnitude here will produce incorrect damping behavior.

  - This is a **significant error** affecting simulation behavior → **-15 points**
