  - The code uses `msection.SetSectionRotation(45 * chrono.CH_RAD_TO_DEG)`.  
  - According to the API, `SetSectionRotation` expects radians, but `CH_RAD_TO_DEG` is a conversion factor from radians to degrees (~57.2958). Multiplying 45 (degrees) by `CH_RAD_TO_DEG` results in a very large angle (~2578 radians), which is incorrect.  
  - The correct usage would be either `45 * chrono.CH_DEG_TO_RAD` (to convert degrees to radians) or simply `chrono.CH_C_DEG_TO_RAD * 45` if defined, or directly use radians.
