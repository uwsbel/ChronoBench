### 2. Correctness (30 points)

- **Use of `SetSectionRotation(45 * chrono.CH_RAD_TO_DEG)`:**  
  `CH_RAD_TO_DEG` is a constant to convert radians to degrees. Multiplying 45 (degrees) by this constant is incorrect. The correct usage would be either:  
  - `45 * chrono.CH_DEG_TO_RAD` (to convert degrees to radians), or  
  - Just `chrono.CH_C_PI / 4` (for 45 degrees in radians).  
  This is a significant API misuse that could cause the rotation angle to be wrong by a factor of ~57.3, affecting beam orientation.
