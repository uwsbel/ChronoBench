### 2. Correctness (30 points)

- **ROS lidar handler registration:**


  ```python
  ros_manager.RegisterHandler(chros.ChROSLidarHandler(lidar, "~/output/lidar/data/pointcloud"))
  ```  
  According to PyChrono API, `ChROSLidarHandler` takes `(sensor, topic)` as arguments, not frequency. Passing frequency as first argument is incorrect and likely causes runtime error or unexpected behavior.  
  **Deduction: 15 points**

  **Deduction: 15 points**
