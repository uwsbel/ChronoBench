### 2. Correctness (30 points)

- **Section Rotation:** The line `msection.SetSectionRotation(45 * chrono.CH_RAD_TO_DEG)` is suspicious. The documentation and typical usage expect radians, but `CH_RAD_TO_DEG` is a conversion factor from radians to degrees. Multiplying 45 (degrees) by `CH_RAD_TO_DEG` (which equals 57.2958...) results in a very large angle (~2578 degrees). This is incorrect and will cause an incorrect section rotation.

  The correct usage should be either:

  - `msection.SetSectionRotation(45 * chrono.CH_DEG_TO_RAD)` (convert degrees to radians), or
  - `msection.SetSectionRotation(chrono.CH_C_DEG_TO_RAD * 45)` (if such constant exists), or
  - Directly pass radians.
