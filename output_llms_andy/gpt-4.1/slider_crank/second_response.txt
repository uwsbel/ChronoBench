## **Errors and Corrections**

### **1. Vector and Quaternion Types**
- **ChVector3d** and **ChFramed** do **not** exist in PyChrono. The correct types are **ChVectorD** and **ChFrameD**.
- **ChAxis_Y** and **ChAxis_X** are not available; use **chrono.VECT_Y** and **chrono.VECT_X**.
- **Q_ROTATE_Y_TO_Z**, **Q_ROTATE_Y_TO_X**, **Q_ROTATE_Z_TO_X** are not available. Use **chrono.Q_from_AngAxis** or **chrono.Q_ROTATE_X_TO_Y**, etc.

### **2. Joint Initialization**
- For **ChLinkLockRevolute** and **ChLinkLockPrismatic**, the third argument should be a **ChFrameD** (not ChFramed).

### **3. Motor Initialization**
- **ChLinkMotorRotationSpeed.Initialize** expects two bodies and a **ChFrameD** (not ChFramed).

### **4. Camera Position**
- The camera should look at the correct position (the piston is at y=0.5, not y=1).

### **5. Miscellaneous**
- Use **chrono.CH_C_PI** for π.
- Use **ChBodyEasyCylinder(axis, radius, height, density)**, but axis should be a vector (e.g., chrono.VECT_Y).
