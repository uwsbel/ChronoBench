### 4. Efficiency (10 points)

- **Unnecessary Calculations:**  
  - The energy calculation loops over all bodies and all pairs of bodies every frame. For large numbers of bodies, this is O(n²) and could be expensive. However, since this is a demo with limited bodies, it is acceptable.  
  - The gravitational force calculation is done manually rather than using a built-in force or force field, but PyChrono does not provide a built-in gravity field for inter-body gravity, so this is acceptable.

- **Optimization Opportunities:**  
  - The list of body pairs is created every frame with `list(combinations(sys.GetBodies(), 2))`. This could be optimized by caching if bodies do not change frequently, but since particles are emitted continuously, the body list changes, so this is acceptable.  
  - The `body.EmptyAccumulators()` is called before force accumulation, which is necessary.

- **Minor inefficiency:**  
  - The gravitational constant is hardcoded inside the loop; better to define once outside (already done).  
  - The `body.GetVelocity().Length2()` is called inside the loop; no issue here.

**Deduction:**  
- 0 points for unnecessary calculations (acceptable given context).
