### 2. Correctness (30 points)

- **Custom ForceFunctor:**  
  - The `AddForce` method is overridden correctly.  
  - However, `AddForce` is called with parameters `(body, pt, w)`. According to PyChrono, `AddForce` should have signature `AddForce(self, body, point, velocity)` or similar. This is consistent.  
  - The force calculation:  
    ```python
    force = -self.spring_coef * (dist - rest_length) - self.damping_coef * diff.Dot(w)
    body.AddForce(force * diff / dist)
    ```  
    This is physically reasonable: spring force proportional to extension, damping proportional to relative velocity projected along the spring direction.  
  - However, `diff.Dot(w)` uses `diff` which is a position difference vector, but `w` is presumably velocity at point `pt`. The velocity vector `w` is in world coordinates, but `diff` is `rel_pos - ground_pt` (both in world coordinates). So the dot product is valid.  
  - The force is applied at the body center (via `AddForce`), which applies force at center of mass. The spring force should be applied at point `pt` (the point on the body where the spring is attached). The API for `AddForce` allows applying force at a point: `AddForce(force, point)`. The code calls `body.AddForce(force * diff / dist)` without specifying the point, so force is applied at COM, which is physically incorrect for a spring attached at a point. This is a **significant correctness issue** (deduct 15 points).  
- **`spring_1` Initialization:**  
  - `spring_1.Initialize(body_1, ground, True, chrono.ChVector3d(0,0,0), chrono.ChVector3d(-1,0,0))`  
  - The last two vectors are the attachment points in local coordinates of body_1 and ground respectively. This is consistent with the API.  
- **`spring_2` Initialization:**  
  - `spring_2.Initialize(body_2, ground, ground_pt, chrono.ChVector3d(1, 0, 0))`  
  - This matches the documented signature for `ChLinkSpring.Initialize(body1, body2, point1, point2)`.  
- **Visualization:**  
  - The camera is placed at `(0,0,6)`, which is behind the bodies (which are at y=-3). This is acceptable but suboptimal for viewing the scene (minor issue).  
- **Gravity:** Set to zero vector `(0,0,0)`. This is intentional and acceptable.  

**Correctness deductions:**  
- Applying spring force at COM instead of at the attachment point: **-15 points**  
- Minor suboptimal camera placement: no deduction under correctness, but noted under visualization.
