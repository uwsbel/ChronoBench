  - Also, `particle.AddGeometry(ChSphere(...))` is invalid; `AddGeometry` is not a method of `ChBody`. Instead, visual shapes or collision shapes are added via the collision model or visual shapes.

  - **Deduction: 15 points** (incorrect and incomplete body creation and collision setup).

  - The script does not call `my_application.Initialize()` or `my_application.SetTimestep()` is called, but this is not a documented method for `ChirrApp`.

  - **Deduction: 15 points** (missing essential component: correct force application).
