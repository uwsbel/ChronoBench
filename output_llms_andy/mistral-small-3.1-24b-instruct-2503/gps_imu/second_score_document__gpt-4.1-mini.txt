  The IMU and GPS sensors are created with correct parameters and filters.

- **Use of `chrono.QUNIT`:**  
  `chrono.QUNIT` is used for the patch orientation, which is correct.

- **Use of `vehicle.GetVehicle().GetMass()`:**  
  This is correct to get the vehicle mass.

- **Use of `manager.Update()`:**  
  Correct to update sensors.

- **Use of `realtime_timer.Spin(step_size)`:**  
  Correct for real-time pacing.

- **Use of `driver.Synchronize(time)`, `terrain.Synchronize(time)`, `vehicle.Synchronize(time, driver_inputs, terrain)`, `vis.Synchronize(time, driver_inputs)`:**  
  Correct synchronization calls.

- **Use of `driver.Advance(step_size)`, `terrain.Advance(step_size)`, `vehicle.Advance(step_size)`, `vis.Advance(step_size)`:**

Overriding inputs manually may conflict with the interactive driver logic. This is a logical inconsistency.

  Correct method to get GPS data.

**Deductions:**  
- 15 points for incorrect use of `chrono.ChFramed` (deprecated/incorrect class for sensor pose).
