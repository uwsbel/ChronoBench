- **Logical errors**:

According to best practices, collision system type should be set before initialization to ensure proper setup. This could cause unexpected behavior or ignored collision system setting.

**Deduction**:  
- Logical error: `driver_inputs` fetched before `driver.Synchronize` → **-10 points**
