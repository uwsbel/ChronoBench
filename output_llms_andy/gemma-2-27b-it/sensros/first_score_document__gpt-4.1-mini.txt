- **Potential issues:**

  - The force applied to the ground body inside the loop: `ground.AddForce(chrono.ChVectorD(100, 0, 0))`. Applying a force to a fixed or static ground body is unusual. The ground body is created with `ChBodyEasyBox` but not explicitly fixed. By default, `ChBodyEasyBox` creates a dynamic body. Ground bodies are typically fixed (`SetFixed(True)`) to prevent movement. Here, the ground is not fixed, so applying force will move it, which is likely unintended.

    This is a **logical error**: ground should be fixed or the force application should be to a dynamic body.

    Deduct **10 points** for logical error.

  - The visualization loop is missing calls to `vis.BeginScene()` and `vis.EndScene()`. According to the API, the rendering loop should be:

    ```python
    while vis.Run():
        vis.BeginScene()
        # simulation steps
        vis.Render()
        vis.EndScene()
    ```

    Missing these calls can cause rendering issues or incomplete frames.

    Deduct **10 points** for incorrect visualization loop usage.
