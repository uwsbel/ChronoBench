According to the API, `ChFramed` is a class representing a coordinate frame, but it is not explicitly documented in the provided doc snippet for use in `AddVisualShape`. Usually, `AddVisualShape` accepts a `ChFrame` or `ChCoordsys` for positioning the shape.
