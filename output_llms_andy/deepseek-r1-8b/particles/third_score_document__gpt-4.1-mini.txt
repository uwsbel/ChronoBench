### 2. Correctness (30 points)

- **Use of `ChContactMaterialNSC` in `ChBodyEasySphere`:**  
  The constructor of `ChBodyEasySphere` expects a material pointer, but `ChContactMaterialNSC` is not a shape material but a contact material. According to PyChrono API, `ChBodyEasySphere` expects a `ChMaterialSurface` or similar.  
  The code uses `sphere_mat = chrono.ChContactMaterialNSC()` and passes it to `ChBodyEasySphere`. This is **incorrect** usage. The correct approach is to create a `ChMaterialSurfaceNSC` and assign friction etc. to it, then pass it to the body.  
  **Major error: -15 points.**

  This is acceptable and correct to disable gyroscopic torque.  
  **No deduction.**

  **No deduction.**
