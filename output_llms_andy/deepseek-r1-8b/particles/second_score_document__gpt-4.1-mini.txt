The potential energy is computed as `-f_attract` where `f_attract` is the gravitational force magnitude. This is **incorrect**: gravitational potential energy between two masses is `-G * m1 * m2 / r`, not `-force`. Force is `G * m1 * m2 / r²`. The code uses force magnitude as potential energy, which is dimensionally and physically incorrect. **Deduct 10 points for logical error in physics calculation.**
