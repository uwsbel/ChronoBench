    - `GetPos()` returns position vector; subtracting previous speed (which is a scalar) from position's X component is nonsensical.
    - The correct approach should be to store previous position or velocity and compute speed as the derivative of position or directly get the chassis body's velocity:
      ```python
      current_speed = vehicle.GetVehicle().GetChassisBody().GetPos().GetX()
      prev_speed = current_speed
      ```
      or better, use `GetVelocity()`:
      ```python
      current_speed = vehicle.GetVehicle().GetChassisBody().GetVelocity().GetX()
      ```
