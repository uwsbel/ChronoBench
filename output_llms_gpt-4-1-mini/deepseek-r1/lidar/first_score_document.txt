  According to the PyChrono API, the constructor signature is:

  ```

  ```

  The parameters appear correct, but the `min_vert_angle` is set to `VERT_FOV/2` (which is positive), which means the vertical scan starts at half the vertical FOV above the horizontal plane. Usually, the vertical FOV is symmetric around zero, so `min_vert_angle` should be negative half the vertical FOV (e.g., `-VERT_FOV/2`) to center the scan vertically. Setting it to positive half will shift the scan upwards, possibly missing the lower half of the vertical FOV.

  This is a **logical error** affecting sensor coverage.
