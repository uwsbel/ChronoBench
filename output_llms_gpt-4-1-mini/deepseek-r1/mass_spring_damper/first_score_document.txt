  - The inertia calculation is incorrect for a cube. The moment of inertia for a cube of mass m and side length a is `(1/6)*m*a²` for each principal axis. The code calculates `(1/12)*(0.2² + 0.2²) = (1/12)*0.08 = 0.006666...` which is actually correct. So the formula is correct, just written differently. So no deduction here.
