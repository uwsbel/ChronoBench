### 2. Correctness (30 points)

- **System creation:**  
  Using `chrono.ChSystem()` instead of `chrono.ChSystemNSC()` or `chrono.ChSystemSMC()` is incorrect and can lead to simulation issues.  
  **Deduct 15 points**

- **Setting contact method:**  
  The code calls `system.SetContactMethod(contact_method)` where `contact_method = chrono.ChContactMethod_NSC`. However, `ChSystem` base class does not have `SetContactMethod()`; this is a method of `ChSystemNSC` or `ChSystemSMC`. This will raise an AttributeError at runtime.  
  **Deduct 15 points**

- **Vehicle initialization:**  
  The vehicles are initialized properly with correct calls and parameters.

- **Visualization synchronization:**  
  The visualization system is synchronized with `driver1.GetInputs()`. However, since two vehicles are present, it would be more consistent to synchronize with both drivers or the system as a whole. This is a minor logical inconsistency but not critical.  
  **Deduct 5 points**

- **Driver synchronization:**  
  Drivers are synchronized with time but have no inputs other than the sinusoidal steering and fixed throttle. This is acceptable.

- **Advancing the system:**  
  The code advances all components correctly.

- **Use of `chrono.QUNIT`:**  
  Correctly used for identity quaternion.

- **Use of `veh.GetDataFile` and `chrono.GetChronoDataFile`:**  
  Correctly used.

**Total deductions for correctness:** 15 (wrong system class) + 15 (calling SetContactMethod on base class) + 5 (minor visualization sync inconsistency) = **35 points**
