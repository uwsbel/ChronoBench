- **Material Friction:**  
  Friction is set as `math.tan(math.radians(30))` which equals approximately 0.577. This is unusual because friction coefficients are typically less than 1 and directly specified, not as tangent of an angle. The comment says "Friction from original 30 degrees" but this is not a standard way to set friction coefficient. Usually, friction coefficient is a scalar value (e.g., 0.6). Using tangent of 30 degrees is not a standard practice and could lead to unexpected friction behavior.
