    `CH_RAD_TO_DEG` is a constant to convert radians to degrees, but the argument is `45 * CH_RAD_TO_DEG`. Since 45 is presumably degrees, multiplying by `CH_RAD_TO_DEG` (which is ~57.2958) results in a very large number (~2578 degrees). The correct usage should be either:
