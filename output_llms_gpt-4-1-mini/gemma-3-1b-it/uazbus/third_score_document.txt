### 2. Correctness (30 points)

  The call `vehicle.SetInitPosition(chrono.ChCoordsysd(initLoc, initRot))` is incorrect because `initLoc` is already a `ChCoordsysd` object, and `ChCoordsysd` constructor expects either a vector and quaternion or no arguments. Passing `initLoc` (a `ChCoordsysd`) as the first argument and `initRot` (a quaternion) as second is wrong. It should be either `vehicle.SetInitPosition(chrono.ChCoordsysd(initLoc.pos, initLoc.rot))` or simply `vehicle.SetInitPosition(initLoc)` if `initLoc` already contains the rotation.
