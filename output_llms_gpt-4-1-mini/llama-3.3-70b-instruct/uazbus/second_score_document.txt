    1. Modify `driver_inputs` (steering, throttle, braking)  
    2. Call `driver.Synchronize(time)`  
    3. Call `vehicle.Synchronize(time, driver_inputs, terrain)`  
    4. Call `terrain.Synchronize(time)`  
    5. Call `vis.Synchronize(time, driver_inputs)`  
  - In the current code, the inputs are modified **after** `driver.Synchronize()` and `vehicle.Synchronize()`, which means the vehicle and driver modules are not receiving the updated inputs for the current timestep. This will cause the vehicle to lag one timestep behind the intended inputs, potentially affecting simulation accuracy.  
  **Deduct 10 points for this logical error.**
