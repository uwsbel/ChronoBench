According to the API, `ChBodyEasySphere` expects a `ChMaterialSurface` or None, but `ChContactMaterialNSC` is a material for contact, not a surface material. This is a subtle but important distinction.  
  **Potential issue:** The material passed may not be the correct type for the body constructor. Usually, `ChBodyEasySphere` uses default material or a `ChMaterialSurface` object.  
  **Deduct 10 points** for incorrect material usage that could affect collision behavior.
