### 2. Correctness (30 points)

- **Body Mass and Fixed Settings:**
  - `mesh_body.SetFixed(False)` but `mesh_body.SetMass(0)`.  
    According to PyChrono best practices, a body with zero mass should be fixed (`SetFixed(True)`), otherwise it leads to undefined behavior or simulation errors.  
    Here, the mesh body is movable (`SetFixed(False)`) but mass=0, which is contradictory and incorrect.  
    **Deduct 15 points** for this significant error.
  - Similarly, `ground_body.SetFixed(False)` and `ground_body.SetMass(0)` is contradictory. Ground should be fixed or have nonzero mass.  
    **Deduct 15 points** for this as well.

- **Lidar Sensor Initialization:**

  - Correctly registered with appropriate topics.
