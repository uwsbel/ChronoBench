    The line:

    This attempts to increment the angle per timestep to move the target along the circular path. However, the units are inconsistent: `step_size` is time (seconds), and `2 * pi * radius` is length (circumference). Dividing time by length does not yield an angle increment.  
    The correct approach is to define a target angular velocity (rad/s) and increment angle by `angular_velocity * step_size`. For example, if the target should complete one circle in T seconds, angular velocity = `2*pi / T`.  
    This is a **logical error** that affects the target movement and steering control.  
    **Deduct 10 points.**
