### 2. Correctness (30 points)

- **Flywheel creation:** Uses `ChBodyEasyCylinder(chrono.ChAxis_Y, 0.30, 0.1, 7800)`. According to the API, `ChBodyEasyCylinder` constructor signature is `(radius, height, density, ...)`. The first argument should be radius, but here `chrono.ChAxis_Y` is passed as first argument. This is incorrect usage. The first argument should be a float radius, not an axis enum.

  *Deduct 15 points for logical error in joint initialization.*

  *Deduct 15 points for logical error.*
