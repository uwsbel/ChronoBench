  - Positions and rotations are set correctly.

This may not correspond directly to the crank angle around the intended axis. A better approach would be to extract the angle around the crank axis (e.g., using `GetRot().Q_to_AngAxis()` or similar). This could cause logical errors in interpreting the crank angle.
