- **Visualization of Sentinel and Target Points:**

  This is **incorrect** usage. According to the PyChrono API, `ChSphereShape` is a visual shape attached to a body or shape asset, not a scene node. To visualize points, one should create a `chrono.ChBody` or `chrono.ChBodyEasySphere` with visualization enabled and add it to the system, or use the Irrlicht API directly to add sphere scene nodes.
