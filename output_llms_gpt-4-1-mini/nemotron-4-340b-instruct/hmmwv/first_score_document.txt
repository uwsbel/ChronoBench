- **Adding vehicle and terrain to system before initialization:**
  - The vehicle and terrain are added to `sys` before calling their `Initialize()` methods.

  - **Deduct 5 points**

  - The API example shows terrain patches added via `terrain.AddPatch(...)` and then `terrain.Initialize()`.

  - **Deduct 15 points**

  - The vehicle and terrain are typically added internally during their `Initialize()` calls.

  - **Deduct 10 points**

- **Use of `chrono.ChRealtimeStep(time_step * real_time_factor)`**
  - The correct function is `chrono.ChRealtimeStep(time_step)`.
