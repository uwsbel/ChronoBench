### 2. Correctness (30 points)

- **Use of `SetSectionRotation`:**  
  The code uses `msection.SetSectionRotation(45 * chrono.CH_RAD_TO_DEG)`.  
  According to PyChrono API, `SetSectionRotation()` expects an angle in radians, not degrees. `chrono.CH_RAD_TO_DEG` is a constant to convert radians to degrees (≈57.2958). Multiplying 45 by `CH_RAD_TO_DEG` converts 45 radians to degrees, which is incorrect. The correct usage should be either:  
  - `msection.SetSectionRotation(45 * chrono.CH_DEG_TO_RAD)` (convert degrees to radians), or  
  - `msection.SetSectionRotation(chrono.CH_C_DEG_TO_RAD * 45)` (if available), or simply pass radians directly.  
  This is a **significant API misuse** and will cause incorrect section rotation.  
  **Deduct 15 points.**
