  ```python

  ```  
  According to the API, `ChBodyEasyBox` signature is:  
  ```python
  ChBodyEasyBox(lengthX, lengthY, lengthZ, density, visualize=True, collision=True, material=None)
  ```  

  The first argument should be `lengthX` (float), not the system. The system is not passed here. Also, the `visualize` and `collision` flags are missing.  
  **Deduct 15 points for incorrect body creation.**

  The ground body is not added to the system via `system.Add(ground)`. This is essential for the body to be part of the simulation.
