  According to the API, `Initialize` expects two bodies and a frame (or two frames) specifying the joint location and orientation. Here, the frames are constructed only with position vectors, no rotation specified. This is allowed but unusual; however, more importantly, the motor connects a beam model (`fea.ChModelBeamIGA`) and a rigid body (`flywheel`). The beam is a FEA model, not a rigid body, and cannot be used directly as a body in a joint. The motor expects `ChBody` objects, not FEA models.

  **Deduct 15 points for incorrect API usage in motor initialization.**
