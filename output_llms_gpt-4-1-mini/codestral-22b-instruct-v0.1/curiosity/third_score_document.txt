The documentation examples use `chrono.ChCoordsysd` or `chrono.ChCoordsys` for initialization. `ChFramed` is not a standard PyChrono class in the API documentation; this could be a mistake or a typo. This could cause an error or unexpected behavior.

- The `ground_mat` is passed to `ChBodyEasyBox` as the material, but `ChBodyEasyBox` expects a `ChMaterialSurface` or similar, not a `ChContactMaterialNSC`. This is a misuse: `ChContactMaterialNSC` is not a material surface but a contact material used internally by the system. The correct approach is to create a `ChMaterialSurfaceNSC` and assign it to the body. This is a significant API misuse that could affect collision behavior.
