### 2. Correctness (30 points)

- **Beam Section Setup:**  
  - `SetSectionRotation(45 * chrono.CH_RAD_TO_DEG)` is suspicious. The documentation and typical usage expect rotation in radians, but `CH_RAD_TO_DEG` is a conversion factor from radians to degrees (~57.2958). Multiplying 45 by this factor converts 45 radians to degrees, which is incorrect.  
  - Likely the intention was to convert degrees to radians, which would be `45 * chrono.CH_DEG_TO_RAD`.  
  - This will cause an incorrect section rotation, affecting beam orientation and simulation results.  
  **Deduct 15 points for incorrect API usage affecting simulation behavior.**
