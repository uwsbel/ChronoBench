  The script calls `hmmwv.SetContactMethod(chrono.ChContactMethod_NSC)` **after** creating the vehicle instance. According to the PyChrono Vehicle API, the contact method should be specified **at construction time** or before initialization to ensure the system is created with the correct contact method. Setting it afterward may not have the intended effect because the underlying system may already be created with a default contact method.  
  **Deduction: 15 points** (incorrect use of contact method setting).
