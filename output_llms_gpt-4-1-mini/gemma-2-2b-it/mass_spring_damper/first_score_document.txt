### 1. Completeness (40 points)

  **Deduction: 10 points**

  The script uses `chrono.ChLinkTSDA(mass=mass, position=(0,0,0), name="Spring")` and then calls `chrono.ChLinkTSDA.connect(...)`.  
  - `ChLinkTSDA` does not accept `mass` or `position` parameters in its constructor.  
  - There is no `connect` class method in `ChLinkTSDA`. Instead, the spring link must be initialized via `Initialize(body1, body2, frameA, frameB)` specifying the two bodies and the frames where the spring attaches.  
  **Deduction: 15 points**

- **Adding Bodies and Links to the System:**  
  The script creates a `world` via `scene_manager.createWorld()` and adds bodies and links to it. This is not consistent with PyChrono API, where bodies and links are added to the system object (`ChSystemNSC` or `ChSystemSMC`).   **Deduction: 15 points**
