  - `my_veh.DoStepDynamics(1e-3)` is called **before** `terrain.Synchronize(time)` and `vis.Synchronize(...)`.  
  According to best practices, synchronization calls should happen **before** advancing dynamics to update the state of terrain and visualization with the current time and inputs.  
  The typical order is:  
  1. Synchronize all modules (driver, terrain, visualization) with current time and inputs.  
  2. Advance dynamics (`DoStepDynamics`).  
  3. Render visualization.  

  Here, the vehicle dynamics step is done first, then terrain and visualization are synchronized. This could cause a one-step lag in terrain and visualization updates, potentially leading to minor inconsistencies in rendering or physics coupling.

  **Deduction: 10 points** (logical error in simulation loop order).

  Minor issue.

  This is correct per API.
