  - The `offset_pose` for the sensors is created using `chrono.ChFramed` but the documentation and typical usage suggest `chrono.ChFrame` or `chrono.ChCoordsys` (depending on version). However, `ChFramed` is a valid class in PyChrono for frames with auxiliary reference. This is acceptable but slightly uncommon.  
  - The `driver.Synchronize(time)` is called before the driver inputs are created in the loop. The driver inputs are created inside the loop but not passed to the driver explicitly. However, the `driver.Synchronize(time)` does not take inputs; inputs are passed to the vehicle. This is correct.

  - The `vehicle.GetSystem().SetCollisionSystemType(chrono.ChCollisionSystem.Type_BULLET)` is called after vehicle initialization. According to best practices, collision system type should be set before initialization. This could affect collision behavior.  
    **Deduct 10 points for this important detail.**
