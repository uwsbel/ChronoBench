According to PyChrono best practices, `GetChronoDataPath()` returns a path with a trailing slash, so concatenating `'vehicle/'` without a path separator may cause an incorrect path (e.g., `.../data/vehicle/` vs `.../datavehicle/`). This should be `os.path.join(chrono.GetChronoDataPath(), 'vehicle')` or ensure the slash is present.  
  **Deduct 5 points for minor inaccuracy in path concatenation.**
