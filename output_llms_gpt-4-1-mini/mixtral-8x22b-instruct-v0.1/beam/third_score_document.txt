### 2. Correctness (30 points)

- **Use of `SetSectionRotation`:**  
  The code calls `msection.SetSectionRotation(45*chrono.CH_RAD_TO_DEG)`.  
  According to typical usage, `SetSectionRotation` expects radians, but here `CH_RAD_TO_DEG` is used, which converts radians to degrees. This is likely a mistake because the constant `CH_RAD_TO_DEG` equals ~57.2958, so multiplying 45 by this yields a very large angle in degrees, which is incorrect.  
  The correct usage should be either `45 * chrono.CH_DEG_TO_RAD` (to convert degrees to radians) or just `chrono.CH_C_PI / 4` for 45 degrees in radians.  
  **Deduct 15 points** for this significant API misuse that could cause incorrect beam orientation.

- **Initialization of `ChLinkMateGeneric`:**  
  The constraints are initialized with `Initialize(hnode3, mtruss, False, hnode3.Frame(), hnode3.Frame())`.

  The boolean `False` indicates not to swap the frames, which is acceptable.

  In Python, lists do not have `.back()` or `.front()` methods; these are C++ STL methods.
