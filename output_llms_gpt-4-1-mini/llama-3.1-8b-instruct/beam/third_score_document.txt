  - The `SetSectionRotation(45*chrono.CH_RAD_TO_DEG)` line is suspicious:  
    `CH_RAD_TO_DEG` is a constant to convert radians to degrees, but the argument is `45 * CH_RAD_TO_DEG`.  
    Since 45 is degrees, multiplying by `CH_RAD_TO_DEG` (which is ~57.2958) would give a very large number (~2578 degrees).  
    The API expects the rotation angle in radians, so the correct usage should be either:  
    - `SetSectionRotation(45 * chrono.CH_DEG_TO_RAD)` (convert degrees to radians), or  
    - `SetSectionRotation(chrono.CH_C_DEG_TO_RAD * 45)` (if such constant exists), or simply provide radians directly.  
    This is a **significant error** that will affect the beam section orientation. (-15 points)
