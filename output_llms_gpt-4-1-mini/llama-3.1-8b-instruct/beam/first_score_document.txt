    - The node is set as static similarly.
  - Collision shapes are created via `chrono.ChCollisionModel.CreateBox()` and `CreateSphere()`, but these methods do not exist in the documented API. Collision shapes are usually created by adding collision models to bodies, not by calling `CreateBox()` directly.

  - **Deduction: 15 points** (incorrect body creation and collision shape setup).
