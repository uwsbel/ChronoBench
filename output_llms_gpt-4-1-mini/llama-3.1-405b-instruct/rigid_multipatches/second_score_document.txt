  - `hmmwv.Initialize()` is called without parameters.

  - The script sets initial position via `SetInitPosition()` before `Initialize()`, which is correct.
  - However, `Initialize()` usually takes the initial position as an argument, e.g., `Initialize(chrono.ChCoordsysd(...))`.

  - **This is a logical error** and can affect simulation behavior.

  - However, the `SetCollision(True)` call on the patch is correct.

  - The script creates `chrono.ChVisualShapeTriangleMesh()` and calls `SetMesh()` with a filename string.
  - According to the API, `SetMesh()` expects a `chrono.ChTriangleMeshConnected` object, not a filename.

    ```python
    mesh = chrono.ChTriangleMeshConnected()
    mesh.LoadWavefrontMesh(veh.GetDataFile("terrain/meshes/Highway_vis.obj"), True, True)
    visual_mesh.SetMesh(mesh)
    ```

  - **Logical error** here.
