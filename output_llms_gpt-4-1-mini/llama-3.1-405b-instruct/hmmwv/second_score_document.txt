  - PID controller logic is reasonable, but the error calculation is questionable:  
    ```python
    error = math.atan2(target_point.y - vehicle_position.y, target_point.x - vehicle_position.x) - vehicle.GetVehicle().GetRot().GetAngle()
    ```  
    Here, `vehicle.GetVehicle().GetRot().GetAngle()` returns the angle of the quaternion representing rotation, but this is not the vehicle's heading angle in the XY plane. The quaternion angle is the rotation angle about the quaternion's axis, which is not necessarily the yaw angle. This will cause incorrect steering error calculation.  
    Correct approach would be to extract the yaw angle (heading) from the vehicle's rotation quaternion, e.g., by converting quaternion to Euler angles or using a helper function.  
    **Logical error: deduct 10 points.**

This is not standard documented usage; typically, visualization systems provide higher-level methods or require custom scene node creation. This may or may not work depending on Irrlicht internals.
