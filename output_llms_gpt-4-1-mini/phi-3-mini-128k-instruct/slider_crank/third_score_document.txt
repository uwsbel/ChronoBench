  The motor joint is initialized with `chrono.ChFramed(crank_center)`.  
  The API expects `chrono.ChCoordsysD` or `chrono.ChCoordsys` for frames, not `ChFramed` (which is a C++ class, not exposed in Python).  
  This is a critical error that will cause failure or incorrect joint setup.  
  Similarly, spherical joints and planar joint are initialized with `chrono.ChFramed(...)` instead of `chrono.ChCoordsysD` or `chrono.ChCoordsys`.  
  This is a significant logical error.  
  **Deduct 15 points.**
