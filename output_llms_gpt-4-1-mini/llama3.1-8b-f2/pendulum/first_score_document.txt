### 2. Correctness (30 points)

- **Incorrect Joint Initialization (-15 points):**  
  The joint is initialized as:  
  ```python
  axis = chrono.ChLine3d(chrono.ChVector3d(0,0,0),chrono.ChVector3d(0,0,1))
  joint = chrono.ChLinkLockRevolute()
  joint.SetName('pendulum_joint')
  joint.Initialize(ground, pendulum, axis, chrono.ChFramed(chrono.ChVector3d(0,0,1)))
  ```  
  According to the API, `ChLinkLockRevolute.Initialize()` expects two bodies and a **frame** (`ChCoordsysD` or `ChCoordsys`), not a `ChLine3d` and a `ChFramed`. The use of `axis` and `chrono.ChFramed` is incorrect. The correct call should be something like:  
  ```python
  joint.Initialize(ground, pendulum, chrono.ChCoordsysD(chrono.ChVector3d(0,0,0), chrono.Q_from_AngAxis(0, chrono.ChVectorD(0,0,1))))
  ```  
  or simply a `ChCoordsysD` with position and rotation specifying the joint frame.
