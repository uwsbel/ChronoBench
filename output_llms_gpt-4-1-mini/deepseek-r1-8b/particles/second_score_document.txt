  The callback inherits from `ChRandomShapeCreator_AddBodyCallback` and overrides `OnAddBody`. It sets texture, binds visualization and collision, and disables gyro torque. This is consistent with API expectations.

  The call to `body.EmptyAccumulators()` is done on all bodies before force calculations, which is unusual. Typically, accumulators are cleared after the step or before applying forces, but here it is done after emission and before energy calculations. This is not necessarily wrong but may be redundant or misplaced.

  Disabling gyro torque on particles is acceptable if intended.
