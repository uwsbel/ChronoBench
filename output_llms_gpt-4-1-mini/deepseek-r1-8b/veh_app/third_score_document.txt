- **Body Creation:**  
  The box and cylinder are created with `ChBodyEasyBox` and `ChBodyEasyCylinder`. However, the cylinder constructor is called as:  
  ```python
  chrono.ChBodyEasyCylinder(chrono.ChAxis_Y, 0.5, 1, 1000)
  ```  
  According to the API, `ChBodyEasyCylinder` expects parameters: radius, height, density, visualize, collision, material (optional). The first argument should be radius (float), not an axis enum. The axis is not a parameter for `ChBodyEasyCylinder`. This is an incorrect usage and will cause an error or unexpected behavior.
