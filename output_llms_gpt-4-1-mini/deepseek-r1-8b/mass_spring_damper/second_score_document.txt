### 2. Correctness (30 points)

- The `MySpringForce` class inherits from `chrono.ForceFunctor` and overrides `ComputeForce`, which is correct in principle. However:  
  - The method returns `chrono.ChVector3d(spring_force, damping_force)`, but `spring_force` and `damping_force` are already `ChVector3d` objects. Passing two vectors as arguments to `ChVector3d` constructor is invalid. The correct approach is to sum the two vectors: `spring_force + damping_force`.  
  - The `spring_force` is computed as `self.spring_coef * (chrono.ChVector3d(0, 0, 0) - local_pos)`, which is a vector from the local position to origin scaled by spring constant. This is reasonable for a linear spring.  
  - The `damping_force` is computed as `self.damping_coef * body.GetMass() * local_vel`. Multiplying damping coefficient by mass and velocity is unusual; damping is usually proportional to velocity only. Multiplying by mass may be incorrect unless justified.  
- The `spring_1` is initialized with `Initialize(body_1, ground, True, chrono.ChVector3d(0, 0, 0), chrono.ChVector3d(-1, 0, 0))`. The third argument `True` is for `pos_are_relative`, which is correct.  
- The `spring_2` is initialized similarly but with the same anchor points as `spring_1`, which is inconsistent given `body_2` position.  
- The simulation loop uses `sys.DoStepDynamics(1e-3)` which is appropriate.  
- Visualization setup is mostly correct.  
- The `SetMass` and `SetInertiaXX` calls are correct.  
- The `EnableCollision(False)` calls are consistent with the example.  

**Deductions:**  
- Incorrect return statement in `ComputeForce` (passing two vectors to `ChVector3d` constructor): **-15 points** (significant API misuse affecting simulation behavior).  
- Multiplying damping coefficient by mass in damping force calculation: **-10 points** (logical error in force calculation).  
- Logical error in `spring_2` initialization anchor points (already deducted under completeness, no double deduction).  
- Minor: No constructor in `MySpringForce` (already deducted).  

**Subtotal Correctness:** 30 - 15 - 10 = **5 points deducted → 25 points**
