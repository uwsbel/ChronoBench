### 2. Correctness (30 points)

- **Inertia Calculation:** The inertia tensor calculation for a cylinder is not quite correct.

  The standard formula for a solid cylinder about its central axis (z-axis) is:

  - \( I_{zz} = \frac{1}{2} m r^2 \)
  - \( I_{xx} = I_{yy} = \frac{1}{12} m (3r^2 + l^2) \)

  The code uses:

  ```python
  Ixx = 0.5 * m * r**2 + (1/12) * m * l**2
  Iyy = Ixx
  Izz = 0.5 * m * r**2
  ```

  This is incorrect because it adds \( \frac{1}{2} m r^2 \) and \( \frac{1}{12} m l^2 \) for Ixx and Iyy, which is not the standard formula.

  Correct Ixx and Iyy should be:

  \[
  I_{xx} = I_{yy} = \frac{1}{12} m (3r^2 + l^2)
  \]

  So the code overestimates Ixx and Iyy by adding an extra \(0.5 m r^2\).

  **Deduct 10 points for incorrect inertia calculation.**
