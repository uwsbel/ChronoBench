According to the API, `ChQuaternionD` expects four components (w, x, y, z) or can be constructed from axis-angle or Euler angles differently.   **Deduct 15 points.**

  The method is called without a time step argument, but the API requires `DoStepDynamics(time_step)`. This will cause the simulation not to advance properly or raise an error.
