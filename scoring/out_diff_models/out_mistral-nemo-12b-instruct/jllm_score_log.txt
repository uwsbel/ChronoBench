    ```python
    body = chrono.ChBody()
    ```

    ```python
    box = chrono.ChBodyEasyBox(1, 1, 1, 1000, True, True, material)
    ```

  - **Example**:
    ```python
    body_aux = chrono.ChBodyAuxRef()
    ```

  - **Usage**: Adds a body to the simulation, making it part of the dynamic system.
