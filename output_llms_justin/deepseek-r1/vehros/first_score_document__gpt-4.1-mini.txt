### 1. Completeness (40 points)

  However, the order of some calls is not optimal:  
  - The vehicle is constructed with `veh.HMMWV_Full(system)` before setting contact method and other parameters. According to best practices, these settings should be done before initialization.  
  - The vehicle is initialized with `hmmwv.Initialize()` before setting visualization types. Visualization types should be set **before** initialization to take effect.  
  **Deduct 10 points** for incorrect configuration order affecting correctness and completeness.

  **Deduct 15 points** for missing or incorrect ROS loop handling (significant omission).
