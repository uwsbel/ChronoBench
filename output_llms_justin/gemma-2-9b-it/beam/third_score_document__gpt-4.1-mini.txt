### 2. Correctness (30 points)

- **Use of `ChNodeFEAxyzrot`:** Correctly initialized with `chrono.ChFramed` and position vectors. ✔️  
- **Beam Section Rotation:** The line `msection.SetSectionRotation(45*chrono.CH_RAD_TO_DEG)` is suspicious. The API expects radians, but `CH_RAD_TO_DEG` is a conversion factor from radians to degrees (≈57.2958). Multiplying 45 by `CH_RAD_TO_DEG` converts 45 radians to degrees, which is incorrect. The argument should be in radians, so either use `chrono.CH_DEG_TO_RAD` or just `45 * chrono.CH_DEG_TO_RAD` to convert degrees to radians. This is a significant API misuse and will cause incorrect section rotation.
