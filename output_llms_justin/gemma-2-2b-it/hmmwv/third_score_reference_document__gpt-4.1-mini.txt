  The assistant's driver class is present but implemented differently. Also, the assistant's driver constructor does not accept a delay parameter but hardcodes delay logic inside `Synchronize`.  
  This is a **significant deviation** in driver implementation and usage of API.  
  **Deduct 10 points** for a component present but lacking important details and deviating from best practice.

  **Deduct 5 points** for minor omission.
