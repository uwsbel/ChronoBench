  - The code computes kinetic energy by iterating over all bodies once, which is efficient.  
  - The gravitational force and potential energy are computed using `combinations(sys.GetBodies(), 2)`, which is O(n²) and unavoidable for pairwise interactions.  
  - However, the code calls `body.EmptyAccumulators()` on all bodies every step, which is necessary.  
  - The gravitational force calculation does not skip very close bodies or optimize with spatial partitioning, but this is acceptable for a demo.  
  - The code calculates `velocity.Length2()` and then multiplies by 0.5 * mass, which is efficient.
