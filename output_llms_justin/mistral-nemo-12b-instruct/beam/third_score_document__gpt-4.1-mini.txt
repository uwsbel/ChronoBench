### 2. Correctness (30 points)

- **Use of `SetSectionRotation(45 * chrono.CH_RAD_TO_DEG)`:**  
  The API expects rotation in radians, but `CH_RAD_TO_DEG` converts radians to degrees. Multiplying 45 by `CH_RAD_TO_DEG` converts radians to degrees, which is incorrect if the function expects radians. The correct usage should be either `45 * chrono.CH_DEG_TO_RAD` or just `chrono.CH_C_DEG_TO_RAD * 45` (if defined), or simply `chrono.CH_C_DEG_TO_RAD` is the conversion factor from degrees to radians. This is a significant API misuse and will affect the beam section orientation. (-15 points)

- **Initialization of `ChLinkMateGeneric` constraints:**  
  The constraints are initialized between nodes (which are FEA nodes, not bodies) and the fixed body `mtruss`. According to the API, `ChLinkMateGeneric.Initialize()` expects two bodies or two frames attached to bodies. Here, `hnode3` and `hnode1` are `fea.ChNodeFEAxyzrot` objects, which are not bodies. This is a misuse of the API and will likely cause runtime errors or undefined behavior. The correct approach is to constrain nodes to bodies via boundary conditions or use appropriate FEA constraints. (-15 points)

- **Force and torque application on nodes:**  
  Applying force and torque directly on nodes is acceptable in FEA, but the force on `hnode2` and torque on `hnode3` are set without any comments or explanation. This is not incorrect but could be clarified.

- **Use of `builder.GetLastBeamNodes().back().GetPos()`:**  
  This is a correct way to get the last node position for building the second beam. ✔️

- **Mesh gravity disabled:** Correctly disables gravity on the mesh. ✔️

- **Solver usage:** Correctly sets Pardiso MKL solver. ✔️

- **Simulation loop:** Correct usage of visualization and stepping. ✔️

**Correctness score:** 30 - 15 (rotation misuse) - 15 (constraint misuse) = **0**
