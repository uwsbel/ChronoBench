  Since `veh.GetDataFile()` prepends the vehicle data path, and the visualization mesh path is `"terrain/meshes/Highway_vis.obj"`, this will resolve to `<vehicle_data_path>/terrain/meshes/Highway_vis.obj`.  
  However, the collision mesh is loaded from `<chrono_data_path>/vehicle/terrain/meshes/Highway_col.obj`.  
  This inconsistency could cause a file not found error if the visualization mesh is not in the vehicle data path.  
  **Deduct 10 points for this inconsistency that could cause runtime errors or confusion.**
