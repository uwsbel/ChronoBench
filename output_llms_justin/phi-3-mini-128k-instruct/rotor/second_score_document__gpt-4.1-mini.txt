  This is a **significant API misuse** that will cause the flywheel creation to fail or behave incorrectly.

According to the API, `Initialize(body1, body2, frame)` is expected, where `frame` is a `ChCoordsys` or `ChFrame` defining the joint frame in absolute coordinates or relative to bodies. Passing just two bodies without a frame is incorrect.

- **Visualization:** Visual shapes are correctly created and added.

- **Solver:** Correctly set.

- **Simulation Loop:** Correct.

- Incorrect `Initialize` call for `ChLinkMateFix` (missing frame argument).
