### 2. Correctness (30 points)

  **Deduction:** 15 points for incorrect API usage.

- **Motor not initialized with `Initialize()`:**

  This is a logical error that will prevent the motor from functioning properly.

  **Deduction:** 10 points for logical error.

- **Use of `builder_iga.GetLastBeamNodes()[65]` and `[32]`:**

  The beam is built with 30 elements, so the number of nodes is `30 + degree` (degree=3), so nodes count is 33. Accessing index 65 is out of range and will cause an error.

  **Deduction:** 15 points for logical error (index out of range).

- **Use of `builder_iga.GetLastBeamNodes().front()`**

  Python lists do not have `.front()` method; this is a C++ style. This will cause an AttributeError.

  **Deduction:** 10 points for logical error.

- **Use of `constr_bb.Initialize(node_top, node_tip, False, node_top.Frame(), node_top.Frame())`:**

  The API documentation shows joints initialized with `Initialize(body1, body2, frame)`. Here, nodes are passed instead of bodies, and frames are passed as `node_top.Frame()`. This is likely incorrect because `ChLinkMateParallel` expects bodies or frames, not nodes directly.

  **Deduction:** 10 points for logical error.

- **Similarly, `constr_cbd.Initialize(node_crnkG, body_crank, False, node_crnkG.Frame(), node_crnkG.Frame())`:**

  Mixing node and body in joint initialization is suspicious and likely incorrect.

  **Deduction:** 10 points for logical error.

- **Use of `chrno.ChTimestepperEulerExplicit(sys)` and setting it as timestepper:**
