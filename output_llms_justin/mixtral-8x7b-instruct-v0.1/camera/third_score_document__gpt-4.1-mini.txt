### 1. Completeness (40 points)

  However, the box is created using `chrono.ChBox(side, side, side, 1000)`, which is **incorrect** because `ChBox` constructor does not take a density parameter (1000). According to the API, `ChBox` is a geometric shape, not a body or easy body.   Also, the body does not have collision enabled (`EnableCollision` is never called), so it will not participate in collisions.  
  **Deduction: 15 points** for incorrect body creation and missing collision setup.
