### 2. Correctness (30 points)

- **Use of `SetSectionRotation(45 * chrono.CH_RAD_TO_DEG)`**:  
  The API expects the rotation in radians. `CH_RAD_TO_DEG` is a constant to convert radians to degrees (≈57.2958). Multiplying 45 (degrees) by `CH_RAD_TO_DEG` converts degrees to degrees * 57.2958, which is incorrect. The correct conversion should be `45 * chrono.CH_DEG_TO_RAD` or simply `chrono.CH_C_DEG_TO_RAD` (if defined). This will cause an incorrect section rotation, affecting beam behavior.  
  **Deduct 15 points** for incorrect API usage that affects simulation behavior.
