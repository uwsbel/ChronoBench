  - The script uses `system = ch.ChronoSystemNSM()`, which is **not a valid class** in the PyChrono API. The correct classes are `ChSystemNSC` or `ChSystemSMC`.
  - **Deduction: 15 points** for missing correct system initialization.

  - **Deduction: 15 points** for incorrect and incomplete body creation and mesh visualization.

    - `chs.SENSOR orientations=[0, 0, 0]` and `chs.SENSOR positions=[0, 0, 1.5]` are not valid keyword arguments or enums in the API.
