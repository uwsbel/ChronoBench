- **Body Creation:**  
  Bodies are created using `ChBodyEasyBox` and `ChBodyEasyCylinder`. However, the usage is incorrect:  
  The constructors are called as `chrono.ChBodyEasyBox(sys, 4, 0.5, 4, 1000, True, chrono.ChVector3d(0, -2, 0))` and similarly for cylinders.  
  According to the API, `ChBodyEasyBox` and `ChBodyEasyCylinder` constructors expect parameters:  
  `(lengthX, lengthY, lengthZ, density, visualize, collision, material=None)`

  This is a **significant error** in body creation.  
  Deduct **15 points** for missing correct body creation.

  Deduct **5 points** for minor API misuse in joint initialization.
