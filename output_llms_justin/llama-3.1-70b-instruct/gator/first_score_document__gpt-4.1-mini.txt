  **Deduct 15 points for missing system stepping, incorrect order of driver input application, and misuse of visualization rendering calls.**

  - Extra closing parentheses in `if application.GetDevice().isKeyPressed(chrono.ChKeyboard::KEY_Left)):`  
  - Use of `chrono.ChKeyboard::KEY_Left` is C++ style; in Python, it should be `chrono.ChKeyboard.KEY_Left`.
