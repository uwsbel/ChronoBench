## Error Analysis:

1. **`chrono.ChRandomShapeCreator_AddBodyCallback`** - This is not a valid base class. The correct approach is to inherit from `chrono.ChRandomShapeCreator` and override its virtual method.

2. **`body.EmptyAccumulators()`** - This method doesn't exist in PyChrono. The correct method is `body.ResetForce()`, `body.ResetTorque()`, or just accumulate forces without clearing since forces should be applied fresh each step.

3. **`chrono.ChZhangDistribution`** - This distribution may not exist in all PyChrono versions. A safer alternative is `chrono.ChNormalDistribution` or using the built-in distributions.

4. **Force accumulation issue** - Calling `EmptyAccumulators()` before force calculation clears all forces including any from the contact solver. Since gravity is set to zero and custom gravity is applied, this might work but is inefficient.

5. **`sys.GetBodies()` inside the loop** - This is inefficient and should be called once before the loop.
