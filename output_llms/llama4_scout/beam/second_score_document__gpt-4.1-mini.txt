**Minor completeness issue:**  
- The beam section rotation is set with `msection.SetSectionRotation(45 * chrono.CH_RAD_TO_DEG)`. According to the API, `SetSectionRotation()` expects radians, but `chrono.CH_RAD_TO_DEG` is a conversion factor from radians to degrees (≈57.2958). Multiplying 45 by this factor converts degrees to radians incorrectly (it actually converts radians to degrees). The correct usage should be either `45 * chrono.CH_DEG_TO_RAD` or just `chrono.CH_C_DEG_TO_RAD` (if defined), or simply `chrono.CH_C_DEG_TO_RAD` constant. This is a correctness issue but also affects completeness since the beam section rotation is an essential parameter.

**Deduction:** 5 points for minor completeness deviation (slight misuse of rotation setting).
