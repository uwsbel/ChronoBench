### 2. Correctness (30 points)

- **Body Mass and Fixed Settings:**
  - `mesh_body` has zero mass and is not fixed. This is physically inconsistent; a zero-mass body should be fixed or given mass.
  - `ground_body` created with `ChBodyEasyBox(1,1,1,1000,False,False)` means density=1000, no visualization, no collision. Then `SetFixed(False)` and `SetMass(0)` override this. This is contradictory: a ground body is typically fixed and should have collision enabled. Here, collision is disabled (False), and the body is not fixed and has zero mass. This will cause the ground to be dynamic but massless and non-colliding, which is illogical and will likely break simulation behavior.

The documentation and typical PyChrono usage prefer `ChCoordsys` or `ChFrame` (depending on version). `ChFramed` is not a standard documented class in PyChrono API; this may be an error or outdated usage. This could cause runtime errors or unexpected behavior.
