  The `box` and `cylinder` are created with `ChBodyEasyBox` and `ChBodyEasyCylinder`. However, the cylinder constructor is called as:  

  According to the PyChrono API, `ChBodyEasyCylinder` expects parameters: radius, height, density, visualize, collision, material (optional). The first argument should be the radius, not an axis enum. The axis is not a parameter for `ChBodyEasyCylinder`. This is an **incorrect use of the API** and will likely cause an error or unexpected behavior.
