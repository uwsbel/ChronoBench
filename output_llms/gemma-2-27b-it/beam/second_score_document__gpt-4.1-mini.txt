### 2. Correctness (30 points)

- **Beam Section Rotation:**  
  `builder.SetSectionRotation(45 * chrono.CH_RAD_TO_DEG)` is incorrect. `CH_RAD_TO_DEG` is a constant to convert radians to degrees (≈57.2958). Multiplying 45 by this constant results in ~2578 degrees, which is nonsensical. The correct usage would be either:  
  - Provide the angle in radians and multiply by `CH_DEG_TO_RAD` (degrees to radians), or  
  - Provide the angle in radians directly.  
  This is a significant API misuse that could drastically affect the beam orientation.  
  - Deduct 15 points.
