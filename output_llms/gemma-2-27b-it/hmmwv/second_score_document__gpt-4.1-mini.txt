  The code calls `vehicle.GetVehicle()` multiple times to access the underlying vehicle object. Since `vehicle` is already a vehicle object, this is redundant and could be simplified by storing `veh_obj = vehicle.GetVehicle()` once. This is minor but affects clarity, not correctness.  
  **No deduction (minor inefficiency, handled under efficiency).**

- **Sentinel and Target Bodies:**  
  These bodies are created as `chrono.ChBody()` but no mass, inertia, or collision is set. This means they are default zero mass and no collision, which is physically incorrect if they are meant to interact physically or be tracked properly. This can affect simulation behavior.  
  **Deduct 15 points** for incorrect body initialization affecting simulation correctness.

  The PID controller input is the difference in x-position only, ignoring y and z. This is a simplification but acceptable for a demonstration.
