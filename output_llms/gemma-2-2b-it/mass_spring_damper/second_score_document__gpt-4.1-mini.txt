### 2. Correctness (30 points)

- **ForceFunctor Implementation:**

  ```python
  def calculate_force(self, body1, body2, relative_position):
      force = self.spring_coef * (relative_position) + self.damping_coef * relative_position
      return force
  ```

  - This is incorrect. The force in a spring-damper system depends on the displacement from rest length and the relative velocity along the spring axis.
  - The `relative_position` argument is a vector, but the force should be proportional to the extension/compression scalar (length difference) times the spring coefficient, and the damping force should depend on relative velocity, not position.
  - Also, the damping term is incorrectly implemented as proportional to position, not velocity.
  - This incorrect force calculation will significantly affect simulation behavior.
