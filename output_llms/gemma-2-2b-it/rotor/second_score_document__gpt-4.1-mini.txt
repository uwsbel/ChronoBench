But the second argument is 2, which corresponds to Z axis. This is inconsistent and likely incorrect. The gravity direction index should be 1 for Y axis. This could cause gravity to be applied incorrectly in the simulation.

  The `ChLinkMateFix.Initialize` method requires two bodies and a frame or two frames. Here, only two bodies are passed without a frame. According to the API, the signature is `Initialize(body1, body2, frame)` or `Initialize(body1, body2, frame1, frame2)`. Passing only two arguments is incorrect and will cause an error or unintended behavior.  
  **Deduct 15 points for incorrect joint initialization.**

  The `ChLinkMateGeneric` is initialized with `builder.GetLastBeamNodes().back()` (which is a node, not a body) and `truss` (body). The API expects bodies for joint initialization, not nodes. This is a logical error. The joint should connect bodies, not nodes.
