- **`SetSectionRotation(45*chrono.CH_RAD_TO_DEG)`**: This is incorrect. The method expects radians, but `CH_RAD_TO_DEG` converts radians to degrees. The correct usage would be either `45 * chrono.CH_DEG_TO_RAD` or simply `chrono.CH_C_DEG_TO_RAD` if defined, or just `chrono.CH_C_DEG_TO_RAD` (not shown in doc) or `chrono.CH_DEG_TO_RAD`.
