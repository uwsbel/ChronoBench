### 2. Correctness (30 points)

- **Custom ForceFunctor Implementation:**  
  - The `MySpringForce` class attempts to override `ComputeForce` but uses `self.spring_coef` and `self.rest_length` which are never defined in the class or passed in. This will cause an AttributeError at runtime.  
  - The force calculation itself is incorrect:
    - The spring force is computed as `self.spring_coef * self.rest_length * (other.GetPos() - body.GetPos())`. The correct spring force should be proportional to the displacement from rest length along the line connecting the two bodies, i.e., something like `-k * (current_length - rest_length) * direction_vector`. Multiplying by rest_length directly is incorrect.  
    - The damping force is computed as `-self.damping_coef * body.GetMass() * body.GetLocalVel() * body.GetLocalVel()`, which is dimensionally and physically incorrect. Damping force should be proportional to relative velocity along the spring direction, not velocity squared or multiplied component-wise.  
    - The return statement constructs a `ChVector3d` mixing `spring_force.x` with `damping_force.y` and `damping_force.z`, which is nonsensical and will produce an incorrect force vector.  
  - This is a **significant error** that will drastically affect simulation behavior.  
- **Spring Initialization:**  
  - `spring_1.Initialize(body_1, ground, True, chrono.ChVector3d(0, 0, 0), chrono.ChVector3d(-1, 0, 0))`  
    - The last two vectors are the connection points in the local frames of the two bodies. For `ground` (fixed at origin), `(-1,0,0)` is fine, but for `body_1` at `(-1, -3, 0)`, the local point `(0,0,0)` may not correspond to the expected attachment point. This may cause the spring to be initialized incorrectly.  
  - Similarly for `spring_2`, the initialization uses the same points but `body_2` is at `(1, -3, 0)`, so the local points do not correspond to the body's position. This is a **logical error** in joint initialization.  
- **Use of `SetFixed(False)` on bodies:** Correct.  
- **Collision Disabled:** Correct for this example.  
- **Mass and inertia set:** Correct.  
- **Visualization shapes:** Correct usage.  
- **Simulation loop:** Correct usage of `vis.Run()`, `BeginScene()`, `Render()`, `EndScene()`, and `sys.DoStepDynamics(1e-3)`.  
- **No error handling:** Minor but see below.  

**Deductions:**
