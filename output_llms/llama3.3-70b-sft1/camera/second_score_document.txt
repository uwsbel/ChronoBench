  `LoadWavefrontMesh` is used correctly with parameters `(filename, False, True)` (no verbose, compute normals).  
  The mesh is scaled by `chrono.ChMatrix33d(2)`. This usage is **incorrect**: `ChMatrix33d` expects a 3x3 matrix, but passing a scalar `2` is not valid. The correct way to scale uniformly is to create a diagonal matrix with 2 on the diagonal, e.g., `chrono.ChMatrix33d(chrono.ChVector3d(2,2,2))`.
