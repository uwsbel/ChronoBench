### 2. Correctness (30 points)

- **Mass and Fixed Settings:**  
  Both `mesh_body` and `ground_body` have `SetMass(0)` but `SetFixed(False)`. According to PyChrono best practices, a body with zero mass should be fixed (`SetFixed(True)`) because zero mass means infinite inertia and no dynamics. Having zero mass and non-fixed leads to undefined or unstable behavior.  
  This is a **significant error**.  
  **Deduct 15 points.**

- **Ground Body:**  
  The ground body is created as a small box (1x1x1) with zero mass and movable. Usually, the ground is fixed and large enough to support sensors and objects. This could cause unexpected sensor behavior or collisions.  
  **Logical error:** -10 points.

- **Use of `SetAngVelParent`:**  
  The code applies angular velocity to the ground body via `SetAngVelParent`. This is a valid method to set angular velocity relative to the parent frame. However, since the ground body is zero mass and non-fixed, this may cause unstable or non-physical behavior.  
  This is related to the above mass/fixed issue, so no additional deduction here.

  **No deduction.**
