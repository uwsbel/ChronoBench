**Minor issues:**  
- The beam section rotation is set with `msection.SetSectionRotation(45 * chrono.CH_RAD_TO_DEG)`. According to the API, `SetSectionRotation()` expects radians, but `chrono.CH_RAD_TO_DEG` is a conversion factor from radians to degrees (≈57.2958). Multiplying 45 by this factor converts 45 radians to degrees, which is incorrect. The correct usage should be either `45 * chrono.CH_DEG_TO_RAD` (to convert degrees to radians) or simply `chrono.CH_C_DEG_TO_RAD` if defined. This is a minor but important detail.  
- The force and torque are applied directly to nodes, which is acceptable in FEA, but no comments or explanation are provided.
