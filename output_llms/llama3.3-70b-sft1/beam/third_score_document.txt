  - The use of `SetSectionRotation(45*chrono.CH_RAD_TO_DEG)` is suspicious: `CH_RAD_TO_DEG` converts radians to degrees, but the argument is 45 (degrees), so multiplying by `CH_RAD_TO_DEG` (which is ~57.2958) results in 45*57.2958 = 2578 degrees, which is incorrect. The API expects radians for rotation angles. The correct usage should be either `45 * chrono.CH_DEG_TO_RAD` or just `chrono.CH_C_DEG_TO_RAD * 45` to convert degrees to radians.
