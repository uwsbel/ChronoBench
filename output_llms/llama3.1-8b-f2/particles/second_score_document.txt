  \[
  U = -G \frac{m_1 m_2}{r}
  \]  
  The code uses \( r^2 \) in the denominator, which corresponds to force magnitude, not potential energy. This is a **significant logical error** affecting energy correctness.  
  **Deduct 10 points for logical error.**
