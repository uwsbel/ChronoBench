### 2. Correctness (30 points)

- **Use of `SetSectionRotation(45*chrono.CH_RAD_TO_DEG)`:**  
  The API expects the rotation angle in radians. `chrono.CH_RAD_TO_DEG` is a constant to convert radians to degrees (≈57.2958). Multiplying 45 by `CH_RAD_TO_DEG` converts 45 radians to degrees, which is incorrect because 45 is presumably degrees.  
  The correct usage should be either:  
  - `SetSectionRotation(45 * chrono.CH_DEG_TO_RAD)` (convert degrees to radians), or  
  - `SetSectionRotation(chrono.CH_C_DEG_TO_RAD * 45)` (if such constant exists), or simply pass radians directly.  
  Using `CH_RAD_TO_DEG` here is a **logical error** that will cause an incorrect rotation angle (very large).
