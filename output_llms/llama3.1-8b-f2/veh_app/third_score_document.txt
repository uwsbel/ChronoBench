- **Body Creation:**  
  The box and cylinder are created with `ChBodyEasyBox` and `ChBodyEasyCylinder`. However, the cylinder constructor is called as:  
  ```python
  chrono.ChBodyEasyCylinder(chrono.ChAxis_Y ,0.5, 1, 1000)
  ```  
  According to the PyChrono API, `ChBodyEasyCylinder` expects parameters: radius, height, density, visualize, collision, material. The first argument should be a radius (float), but here `chrono.ChAxis_Y` (an enum or vector) is passed as the first argument. This is **incorrect usage** and will cause an error or unexpected behavior.  
  **Deduct 15 points.**
