### 2. Correctness (30 points)

- **Force Application on Node:**  
  The force is applied directly to the front node with `SetForce(chrono.ChVector3d(0, -0.7, 0))`. This is acceptable, but it applies a constant force rather than gravity. The comment says "moving under gravity alone," but gravity is not explicitly set in the system or applied as a force. This is a **logical error**: gravity should be set on the system (`sys.Set_G_acc(...)`) or applied as a force on all nodes/bodies. Deduct 10 points.

- **Truss Body Setup:**  
  The truss body is created and fixed (`SetFixed(True)`), but it is never added to the system (`sys.Add(mtruss)` is missing). This means the hinge constraint is initialized with a body not in the system, which is incorrect and will cause simulation errors or undefined behavior. Deduct 15 points for incorrect API usage.

  The revolute joint `ChLinkLockRevolute` is initialized with `Initialize(builder.GetLastBeamNodes().back(), mbody_box, False, False)`. According to the documentation, `Initialize` for `ChLinkLockRevolute` expects two bodies and a coordinate system frame (`ChCoordsysD`) or a frame object, not booleans. Using `False, False` is incorrect and will cause errors or unexpected behavior. Deduct 15 points.

  The box body is set with zero mass but nonzero inertia. Physically, a zero-mass body should not have inertia defined, and this can cause solver issues. Usually, fixed bodies have zero mass and inertia, or dynamic bodies have both mass and inertia. This is a **logical inconsistency**. Deduct 10 points.

  Applying force only on the front node of the beam may not be physically consistent with gravity acting on the entire beam. Gravity should be set globally or applied as distributed loads. Minor issue but related to the earlier gravity omission. Deduct 5 points.
