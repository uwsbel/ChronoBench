  Joints like `ChLinkMateParallel`, `ChLinkMatePrismatic`, and `ChLinkMateGeneric` are initialized with nodes (`node_top`, `node_tip`, etc.) instead of bodies. According to the API, joints should be initialized between bodies, not nodes. Nodes are part of the FEA mesh and do not represent rigid bodies.
