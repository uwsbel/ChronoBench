### 2. Correctness (30 points)

- **System Initialization:**  
  Correct use of `ChSystemSMC()` for smooth contact.

- **Beam Section Setup:**  
  - `SetSectionRotation(45*chrono.CH_RAD_TO_DEG)` is suspicious:  
    - `CH_RAD_TO_DEG` is a constant to convert radians to degrees.  
    - The argument is `45 * CH_RAD_TO_DEG`, which converts 45 radians to degrees, but 45 radians is ~2578 degrees, which is likely unintended.  
    - The user probably meant to convert 45 degrees to radians, which would be `45 * chrono.CH_DEG_TO_RAD`.  
  This is a significant API misuse and will affect beam orientation.  
  **Deduct 15 points.**

- **Node Forces and Torques:**
