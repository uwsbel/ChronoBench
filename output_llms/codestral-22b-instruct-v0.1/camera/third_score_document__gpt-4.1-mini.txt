However, `update_rate`, `image_width`, `image_height`, `fov`, `lag`, and `exposure_time` are global variables defined after the `main()` function. This is a logical error because these variables are used before they are defined. In Python, this will cause a `NameError` unless the variables are global and the function is called after their definition (which is the case here).
