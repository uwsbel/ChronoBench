### 1. Completeness (40 points)

This is dimensionally inconsistent for a gravitational force (should divide by distance squared, multiply by unit vector). Also, the force is returned but never applied.
  - **Deduct 15 points** for incorrect force implementation and application.

  - The simulation loop uses `app.DoStep()` instead of `app.DoStepDynamics(time_step)` or `my_system.DoStepDynamics(time_step)`. The documentation suggests `DoStepDynamics` for advancing simulation.
