- **Logical Errors:**  
  The lane change and braking logic modifies `driver_inputs` directly after calling `driver.GetInputs()`. However, `driver.GetInputs()` returns a copy of the inputs, not a reference to the driver's internal state. Modifying this copy will not affect the driver's actual inputs unless `driver.SetInputs()` or a similar method is called, which is missing. This is a logical error that will prevent lane change and braking commands from taking effect.
