### 2. Correctness (30 points)

- **Beam Section Rotation:**  
  `msection.SetSectionRotation(45 * chrono.CH_RAD_TO_DEG)`  
  `CH_RAD_TO_DEG` is a constant to convert radians to degrees (≈57.2958). Multiplying 45 by this converts 45 radians to degrees, which is incorrect because the function expects radians. If the intent was 45 degrees, it should be converted to radians by multiplying by `CH_DEG_TO_RAD` (~0.01745). This will cause incorrect beam section rotation, affecting simulation results significantly.  
  **Deduct 15 points.**
