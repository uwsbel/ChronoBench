According to PyChrono API and best practices, joints should be initialized between **bodies**, not between nodes and bodies. Nodes are not rigid bodies and cannot be connected directly with `ChLinkMateFix` or other `ChLink` objects. Instead, nodes are part of the FEA mesh and do not have rigid body dynamics.  
  This is a **significant logical error** that would cause the simulation to fail or behave incorrectly.
