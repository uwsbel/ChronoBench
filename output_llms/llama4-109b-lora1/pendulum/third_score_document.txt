- **Joint Initialization Frames:**  
  The revolute joints are initialized with frames at `(0,0,1)` position and identity quaternion `(1,0,0,0)`. This means the joint axis is at z=1 in absolute coordinates. Usually, revolute joints are placed at the pivot point, which for a pendulum would be at the pendulum's pivot location (likely at the pendulum's end or the connection point). Here, the joint frame is at `(0,0,1)`, which is the same for both joints. This may cause incorrect joint behavior because the joints are not placed at the pendulum ends or the correct pivot points.

  - The first pendulum is positioned at `(1,0,1)`, the joint is at `(0,0,1)`. The joint frame is not coincident with the pendulum's position, which is unusual.

  - The second joint is initialized at `(0,0,1)` again, connecting pendulum1 and pendulum2, but pendulum2 is at `(1.5,0,1)`.

  This is a **logical error** in joint initialization, as the joint frames should be located at the actual pivot points between bodies to correctly constrain their relative motion.

  **Deduct 10 points for logical error in joint initialization.**
