### 2. Correctness (30 points)

- **`chrono.SetChairsThreading(True)`** is called at the start. This function is deprecated or not part of the documented API; the correct threading setup is usually handled internally or via system settings. This call is unnecessary and may cause confusion or errors.  
  **Deduction: 5 points**

- **`chrono.SetChronoDataFilePath(chrono.GetChronoDataFilePath() + '/vehicle/hmmwv/')`**  
  This is a misuse of the API. The correct function is `chrono.SetChronoDataPath(path)`, not `SetChronoDataFilePath`. Also, `GetChronoDataFilePath()` is not a documented function; the documented function is `GetChronoDataFile(filename)`.

  **Deduction: 15 points**
