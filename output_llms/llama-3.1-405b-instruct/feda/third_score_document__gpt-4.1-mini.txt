### 2. Correctness (30 points)

- **Potential Issues**:  
  - Setting the collision system type after vehicle initialization might cause the system to not properly configure collision detection. According to best practices, collision system type should be set before vehicle initialization. This could lead to subtle simulation inaccuracies or performance issues.
