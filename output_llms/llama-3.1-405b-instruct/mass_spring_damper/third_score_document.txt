Since `body_1` is at `(-1,-3,0)`, the relative point `(0,0,0)` is at the body's center, and `(-1,0,0)` on ground is offset by -1 in x. This means the spring connects the center of `body_1` to a point on ground at `(-1,0,0)`. The rest length is set to 1.5, which is less than the initial distance (~3 units), so the spring will be under tension initially.

  This is not necessarily incorrect but could be a logical oversight depending on the intended behavior.
