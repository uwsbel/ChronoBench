### 2. Correctness (30 points)

- **Use of `ChLinkTSDA.Initialize`:** The method is called with `(body1, body2, True, chrono.ChVector3d(...), chrono.ChVector3d(...))`.  
  According to the documentation, `Initialize` expects `(body1, body2, bool, pos1, pos2)` where `pos1` and `pos2` are the attachment points in the local coordinate systems of the two bodies.  
  Here, the points are given as absolute positions in world coordinates (e.g., `chrono.ChVector3d(0,0,0)` and `chrono.ChVector3d(-1,0,0)`), which is incorrect. The points should be specified in the local coordinate frames of the respective bodies. This will cause incorrect spring attachment points and thus wrong simulation behavior.

- **Visual Shape Creation:**  
  - `chrono.ChVisualShapeBox(1,1,1)` is called with three separate floats instead of a `ChVector3d` as per the documentation. The documentation shows `ChVisualShapeBox(chrono.ChVector3d(1,1,1))`. This is a minor API misuse but likely tolerated by PyChrono (depends on implementation).  
  - Similarly, `chrono.ChVisualShapeSphere(0.1)` is correct.

- **Spring Visual Shape:**  
  Adding `ChVisualShapeSpring` to the spring link is correct.

- **Body Mass and Inertia:**  
  Mass and inertia are set properly.

- **Ground Body:**  
  The ground is fixed and collision disabled, which is correct.

- **Gravity:**  
  Gravity is set to zero, which is acceptable if intended.

- **Visualization Camera Position:**  
  Camera is at `(0,0,6)`, which is behind the bodies positioned at y = -3. This is acceptable but might not give the best view.

- **Simulation Time Step:**  
  `sys.DoStepDynamics(1e-3)` is a reasonable time step.

**Deductions:**  
- Incorrect use of `ChLinkTSDA.Initialize` with world coordinates instead of local coordinates is a **significant error** affecting simulation correctness. Deduct **15 points**.
