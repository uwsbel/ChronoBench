### 2. Correctness (30 points)

- **API Usage:**

  - Loading mesh with `LoadWavefrontMesh()` is correct, but the `Transform()` call uses `ch.ChMatrix33d(1)` which is invalid; `ChMatrix33d` expects a 3x3 matrix, but passing `1` is incorrect.   - Sensor setup appears correct, with proper filters and names.  
  - ROS handlers are registered properly.

- **Logical errors:**  
  - The ground body is set as non-fixed (`SetFixed(False)`) but with zero mass, which is inconsistent; typically, static bodies are fixed (`SetFixed(True)`) and do not need mass.  
  - The `SetAngVelParent()` call on `ground_body` is suspicious; `SetAngVelParent()` is not a standard method in the API; perhaps intended to set angular velocity, but the correct method is `SetAngVel()`.  
  - The simulation loop calls `sys.GetChTime()` but does not set initial `time` explicitly; it starts at 0, which is fine.  
  - No joints or constraints are added, but that is acceptable if the focus is sensor setup.

- **API misuses:**  
  - The `Transform()` method's argument is incorrect; passing a scalar `1` instead of a proper matrix.  
  - The `SetMass(0)` on a dynamic body is questionable; typically, fixed bodies are used for static parts.  
  - The `SetAngVelParent()` method does not exist; should be `SetAngVel()`.

**Total deductions for correctness:** 15 points (incorrect `Transform()` usage, inconsistent mass/fixed settings, and incorrect method call).
