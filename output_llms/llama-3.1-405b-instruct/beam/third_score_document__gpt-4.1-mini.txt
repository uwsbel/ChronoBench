### 2. Correctness (30 points)

- **Use of `SetSectionRotation(45*chrono.CH_RAD_TO_DEG)`:**  
  `CH_RAD_TO_DEG` converts radians to degrees, so multiplying 45 (degrees) by this constant is incorrect. It should be either `45 * chrono.CH_DEG_TO_RAD` (to convert degrees to radians) or just `chrono.CH_C_DEG_TO_RAD` if available. This is a significant API misuse that could affect beam orientation. (-15 points)

- **Initialization of `ChLinkMateGeneric` constraints:**  
  The constraints are initialized between nodes (FEA nodes) and a rigid body `mtruss`. According to PyChrono API, `ChLinkMateGeneric` is designed for bodies, not nodes. Nodes are part of the FEA mesh and do not behave like rigid bodies. This is a logical error and likely to cause simulation issues or crashes. The correct approach is to constrain nodes via boundary conditions or fix nodes directly, not via `ChLinkMateGeneric`. (-15 points)

- **Force and torque application on nodes:**  
  Applying forces and torques directly on nodes is acceptable in FEA, but no time integration or solver settings are adjusted accordingly. This is acceptable but could be better documented.

- **`mesh.SetAutomaticGravity(False)`:**  
  This disables gravity on the mesh, which is fine if intentional. No deduction here.

- **Use of `builder.GetLastBeamNodes().back()` and `.front()` to set fixed and force:**  
  This is correct usage.
