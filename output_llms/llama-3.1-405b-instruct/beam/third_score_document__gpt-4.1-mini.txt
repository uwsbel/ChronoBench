According to the API, `SetSectionRotation` expects radians, but `CH_RAD_TO_DEG` is a constant to convert radians to degrees (≈57.2958). Multiplying 45 by `CH_RAD_TO_DEG` converts 45 radians to degrees, which is incorrect. The intended rotation is likely 45 degrees, so it should be converted to radians by multiplying by `CH_DEG_TO_RAD` or simply use `chrono.CH_C_DEG_TO_RAD` if available.
