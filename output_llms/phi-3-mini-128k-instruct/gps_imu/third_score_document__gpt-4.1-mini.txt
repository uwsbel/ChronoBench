### 1. Completeness (40 points)

  GPS data is logged and plotted at the end.

- The `driver.SetSteeringDelta(0.0)`, `SetThrottleDelta(0.5)`, and `SetBrakingDelta(0.0)` are called before `driver.Initialize()`, which is acceptable but could be better documented.

- The vehicle's collision system is set to BULLET after initialization, which is acceptable but could be set before initialization for clarity.

  The synchronization and advancing calls follow the expected pattern.

- **Potential Issues:**
