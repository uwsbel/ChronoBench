Also, `spring` and `damper` are created as rigid bodies with zero mass and some properties like `SetRestLength()` and `SetDamping()`, which do not exist for rigid bodies. Typically, a `ChLinkTSDA` connects exactly two bodies, initialized with `Initialize(body1, body2, frame1, frame2)`.
