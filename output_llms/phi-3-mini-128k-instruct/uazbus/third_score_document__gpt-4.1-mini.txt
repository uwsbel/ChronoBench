  - The simulation loop advances the simulation twice per iteration, once before setting `driver_inputs.throttle = 0.5` and once after. This means the first advance uses whatever inputs the driver sets, and the second advance uses a forced throttle of 0.5. This is logically inconsistent and could cause unexpected behavior.
