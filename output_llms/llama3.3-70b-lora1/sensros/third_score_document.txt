### 2. Correctness (30 points)

- **Mass and Fixed State:**  
  Setting mass to zero but `SetFixed(False)` on both mesh_body and ground_body is incorrect and can cause simulation errors or undefined behavior. Static bodies should be fixed (`SetFixed(True)`) or have nonzero mass if movable.  
  **Deduct 15 points** for this significant error.

- **Use of `SetAngVelParent`:**  
  The method `SetAngVelParent` is called on `ground_body`. According to PyChrono API, the correct method to set angular velocity is `SetWvel_par()` or `SetWvel_loc()`. `SetAngVelParent` is not a standard method in PyChrono API. This likely will cause an AttributeError or no effect.  
  **Deduct 15 points** for incorrect API usage.

  The mesh is transformed with identity rotation and zero translation, which is redundant but harmless.  
  **No deduction.**
