    ```python
    ChLidarSensor(parent, offsetPose, h_fov, v_fov, points_per_scan)
    ```  
    or similar. The parameters here seem to be:  
    - parent: vehicle.GetVehicle()  
    - offsetPose: chrono.ChVector3d(0,0,1.5) (should be a `ChCoordsys` or `ChFrame` pose, not just a vector)  
    - 360, 10, 5: likely horizontal FOV, vertical FOV, and points per scan or scan frequency.  
    This is a potential API misuse: the offset pose should be a `ChCoordsys` or `ChFrame` (position + rotation), not just a vector. This could cause errors or unexpected behavior.
