This is physically incorrect: gravitational potential energy between two masses is `-G * m1 * m2 / r`, not the force magnitude. The code calculates force magnitude and subtracts it as potential energy, which is a logical error.

The correct method to clear forces is `body.Empty_forces_accumulators()` or `body.Empty_forces_accumulators()` is not standard; the documented API does not mention `EmptyAccumulators()`.
