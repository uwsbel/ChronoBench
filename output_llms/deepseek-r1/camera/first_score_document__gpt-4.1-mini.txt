
  The documentation for `Set_A_axis` expects three orthonormal vectors representing the columns of the rotation matrix in order: X, Y, Z axes.  
  Here, `right` is X, `up` is Y, and `forward` is Z, which is correct. No deduction.

- **`SetRot` with quaternion:**  
  `rot_matrix.Get_A_quaternion()` returns a `ChQuaternionD` suitable for `SetRot`. Correct usage. No deduction.

  Indexing is done as `img_data[image_height//2, image_width//2]`.
