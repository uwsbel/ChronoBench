#### 1. Completeness (40 points total)

- **Component Configuration**: 
  - The material properties and mesh creation are adequately defined, but the monitoring of nodes could be more explicit in terms of what is being monitored and why. **(Deduct 5 points)**

**Total Deductions for Completeness: 25 points**

#### 2. Correctness (30 points total)
- **Incorrect Use of API**: 
  - The use of `SetNodes` for the elements appears correct, but the boundary conditions for the nodes are not well-defined, which could lead to unexpected simulation behavior. **(Deduct 10 points)**
- **Logical Errors**: 
  - The fixing of upper nodes is done for a specific range (30x30), which may not correspond to the actual mesh size defined by `nsections_x` and `nsections_z`. This could lead to an out-of-bounds error if `nsections_x` or `nsections_z` is less than 30. **(Deduct 10 points)**
- **Minor Inaccuracies**:

**Total Deductions for Correctness: 25 points**
