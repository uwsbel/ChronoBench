- **`sys.DoStaticLinear()` is called before the simulation loop:** This is acceptable if a static linear analysis is intended before dynamics, but the system is a dynamic system (`ChSystemSMC`), so this may be a misuse or at least unusual.
- **`sys.SetGravitationalAcceleration()` is called after enabling automatic gravity on the mesh:** This is acceptable but redundant.
