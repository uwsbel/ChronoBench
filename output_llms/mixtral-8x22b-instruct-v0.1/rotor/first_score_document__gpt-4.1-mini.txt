- **Flywheel Inertia Calculation:**  
  The inertia is calculated as:  
  ```python
  flywheel_inertia = 0.5 * flywheel_density * np.pi * (flywheel_radius**4 - (flywheel_radius - flywheel_thickness)**4)
  ```  
  This formula is dimensionally incorrect for inertia (units mismatch). The moment of inertia for a solid cylinder is:  
  \[
  I = \frac{1}{2} m r^2
  \]  
  For a hollow cylinder, it is:  
  \[
  I = \frac{1}{2} m (r_{outer}^2 + r_{inner}^2)
  \]  
  The code uses a volume-like term inside the inertia calculation, which is incorrect. Also, the mass is computed separately. The inertia should be computed from mass and geometry, not density and volume to the fourth power.  
  **Deduct 10 points** for logical error in inertia calculation.
