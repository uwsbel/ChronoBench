  - `AddVisualShape` is called on `ground` with `ChVisualShapeSphere(0.1)` and a `ChFramed` frame.
  - The `ChFramed` constructor is called with a `ChVector3d` argument, but per PyChrono API, `ChFramed` expects a `ChCoordsys` or `ChCoordsysD` (position + rotation). This is a minor API misuse.
- **Body addition:**
  - `sys.AddBody()` is used instead of `sys.Add()`. The documentation shows `sys.Add(body)`. This is a minor inconsistency but likely acceptable as `AddBody` is an alias or older method.
- **Mass and inertia:**
  - Mass and inertia are set correctly.
- **Collision:**
  - Collision is disabled on all bodies, which is acceptable for this demo.
- **Visualization camera:**
  - Camera is placed at `(0,0,6)`, which is behind the bodies at y=-3, which may not give the best view but is not incorrect.
- **Simulation step size:**
  - `1e-3` is a reasonable time step.

**Deductions:**

- **10 points** for minor API misuse in `ChVisualShapeBox` constructor (should be a vector, not three floats).
- **5 points** for minor misuse of `ChFramed` constructor (should be `ChCoordsys` or `ChCoordsysD`).
