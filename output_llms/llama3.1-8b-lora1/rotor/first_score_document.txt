  - `shaft.GetFirstBody()` and `shaft.GetSecondBody()` are used, but `ChShaft` does not have these methods. These are methods of `ChLinkShaft`.

  - `flywheel.AddCollisionShape(chrono.ChCollisionShapeBox(m, 0.1, 0.1, 0.1))` is incorrect; `ChCollisionShapeBox` constructor expects size vector, not mass as first argument.
  - The beam creation in `main()` uses `fea.ChIGABeamFEA(shaft.GetFirstBody(), shaft.GetSecondBody(), 0.01, 0.1, 100, 100)` which is invalid because `shaft` is not a link between bodies.
