- **Use of `chrono.VECT_Y` and `chrono.VECT_Z`:** The API doc uses `chrono.VECT_Y` and `chrono.VECT_Z` as constants, so this is correct.

**Deduction:**

- 15 points for incorrect motor function call (`SetMotorFunction` instead of `SetSpeedFunction`).
