### 2. Correctness (30 points)

  This is not a documented method of `veh.RigidTerrain`. Boxes are `chrono.ChBox` objects (visual shapes), not bodies. Adding visual shapes to terrain patch is not correct. To add boxes as physical objects, one should create `chrono.ChBody` objects (e.g., `ChBodyEasyBox`) and add them to the system.  
  This is a **significant API misuse** that will affect simulation behavior (boxes won't be simulated physically).  
  **Deduct 15 points.**

- **Box Creation**:  
  Boxes are created as `chrono.ChBox` (visual shapes), not as bodies. This means they have no dynamics or collision.  
  **Deduct 10 points** for logical error in body creation.
