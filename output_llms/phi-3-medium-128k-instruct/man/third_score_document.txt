  However, the **random boxes** are created as `chrono.ChBox` objects and added to the terrain via `terrain.AddBox(box)`. According to the API, `ChBox` is a visualization shape, not a physical body. To add physical boxes to the simulation, one should create `chrono.ChBody` objects (e.g., `ChBodyEasyBox`) and add them to the system. Using `terrain.AddBox(box)` is not a documented or standard way to add physical boxes to the simulation. This means the boxes are likely not physical bodies and will not interact physically.
