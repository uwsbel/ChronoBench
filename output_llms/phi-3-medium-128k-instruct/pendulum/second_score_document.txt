  The spherical joint is created as `chrono.ChLinkSpherical()`, but the `Initialize()` method is called with `(ground, pend_1, chrono.ChVector3d(0, 0, 1))`. According to the API, `Initialize()` expects `(body1, body2, frame)` where `frame` is a `ChCoordsys` or `ChFrame` specifying the joint coordinate system in absolute coordinates. Passing a `ChVector3d` alone is incorrect and will cause an error or unexpected behavior.  
  **Deduct 15 points** for this significant API misuse.
