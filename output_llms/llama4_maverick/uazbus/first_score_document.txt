### 2. Correctness (30 points)

  The script calls `uazbus_vehicle.Advance(time)`, `terrain.Advance(time)`, `driver.Advance(time)`, and `vis.Advance(time)`. According to the PyChrono vehicle API, `Advance()` expects a time step (delta time), not the absolute simulation time. Here, `time` is obtained as `uazbus_vehicle.GetSystem().GetChTime()`, which is the absolute simulation time, not a time step. This is a significant misuse that will cause incorrect simulation advancement (likely advancing by a large and increasing amount each frame).  
  **Deduction: 15 points**

  There is no explicit time step defined or used in the simulation loop. The standard practice is to define a fixed time step (e.g., 0.01 s) and advance all components by that step.
