The `Initialize` method for `ChLinkMateGeneric` expects two bodies or physics items, not nodes.

Using `ChLinkMateGeneric` between a node and a body is incorrect and will likely cause runtime errors or undefined behavior.  
  - The force and torque are applied directly to nodes, which is correct.  
  - `SetSectionRotation` misuse as noted above (degrees vs radians). This affects beam orientation and simulation correctness.
