- **Rigid Bodies Creation:**  
  The box and cylinder are created with `ChBodyEasyBox` and `ChBodyEasyCylinder`. However, the cylinder constructor is called as:  
  ```python
  chrono.ChBodyEasyCylinder(chrono.ChAxis_Y ,0.5, 1, 1000)
  ```  
  According to PyChrono API, `ChBodyEasyCylinder` expects parameters: radius, height, density, visualize, collision, material (optional). The first argument should be radius (float), but here `chrono.ChAxis_Y` (an enum or axis specifier) is passed as the first argument, which is incorrect. This will cause an error or unexpected behavior.  
  **Deduct 15 points for incorrect API usage of `ChBodyEasyCylinder`.**
