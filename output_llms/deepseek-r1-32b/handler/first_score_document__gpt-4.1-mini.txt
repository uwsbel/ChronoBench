### 2. Correctness (30 points)

- **Floor creation:** Using `chrono.ChCollisionModel()` directly as a body is incorrect. The floor should be a `ChBody()` with collision shapes added to its collision model. Also, the collision model needs to be built with `BuildModel()`. **-15 points**
