### 2. Correctness (30 points)

- **Incorrect method `SetBodyFixed(True)` used instead of `SetFixed(True)` for the ground body:**  
  This will cause the ground not to be fixed, leading to incorrect simulation behavior.  
  **Deduct 15 points**

- **Spring initialization with identical points for both ends:**  
  The spring should be initialized with the connection points in the local frames of the two bodies. Using the same point for both ends is logically incorrect and will cause the spring to have zero length or undefined behavior.  
  **Deduct 15 points**

- **Use of `SetSpringFilinear` and `SetDampingFilinear`:**  
  These methods do not exist in the documented API. The correct methods are `SetSpringCoefficient()` and `SetDampingCoefficient()`.  
  **Deduct 15 points**

- **Use of `vis.Draw()`, `vis.Render()`, and `vis.Wait()` in the simulation loop:**  
  These methods are not part of the documented visualization API. The correct rendering loop involves `vis.BeginScene()`, `vis.Render()`, and `vis.EndScene()`.  
  **Deduct 10 points**

- **Adding assets to `spring` (a link) instead of to bodies:**  
  Visual assets should be added to bodies, not to links. Adding visual assets to links is not standard and may not render correctly.  
  **Deduct 10 points**

- **Adding color assets to `vis` instead of bodies:**  
  `AddAsset()` is called on `vis` with color assets, which is not correct. Color assets should be added to bodies or shapes.
