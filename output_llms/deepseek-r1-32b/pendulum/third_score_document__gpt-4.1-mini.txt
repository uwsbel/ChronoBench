### 2. Correctness (30 points)

  - Visualization calls are correct.

  - The joint initialization using `ChFramed` instead of `ChCoordsysD` is a logical/API error.  
  - The joint frames' positions for the second revolute joint are set at `(2, 0, 1)` which is relative to the global frame, but the joint is between `pend_1` and `pend_2`. Usually, joint frames should be specified in the coordinate system of the bodies or as a global coordinate system consistent with the bodies' positions. This may cause unexpected joint behavior.
