### 2. Correctness (30 points)

- **Use of `ChContactMaterialNSC` in `ChBodyEasySphere`:** The constructor expects a material pointer, but the code creates a `ChContactMaterialNSC()` and passes it directly. According to PyChrono API, `ChBodyEasySphere` expects a `ChMaterialSurface` or `ChMaterialSurfaceNSC` object, not a `ChContactMaterialNSC`. `ChContactMaterialNSC` is used for contact between two materials, not as a material for a body. This is a significant misuse. The correct approach is to create a `ChMaterialSurfaceNSC` and assign friction etc. to it.  
  **Deduct 15 points** for incorrect material usage.

- **Energy Calculation:**  
  - Kinetic energy is calculated as `0.5 * mass * (force length squared)`. This is incorrect. Kinetic energy depends on velocity, not force. The correct formula is `0.5 * mass * velocity.Length2()`.  
  - Using accumulated force for kinetic energy is a logical error.  
  **Deduct 10 points** for logical error in energy calculation.

- **Potential Energy Calculation:**  
  - The potential energy is computed as `-f_attract` where `f_attract` is the magnitude of the gravitational force between bodies. This is incorrect. Gravitational potential energy between two masses is `-G * m1 * m2 / r`, not force magnitude.  
  - The code uses force magnitude as potential energy, which is a logical error.  
  **Deduct 10 points** for logical error in potential energy calculation.

- **Use of `body.EmptyAccumulators()` inside the loop:**  
  - This is unusual and likely incorrect. The accumulators (forces, torques) are cleared internally by the system during the step. Manually clearing them before stepping may interfere with force accumulation.  
  - However, since the code calls it after particle emission and before stepping, it may be redundant or unnecessary. Minor issue.  
  **Deduct 5 points** for unnecessary API call.

- **Use of `SetUseGyroTorque(False)` on bodies:**  
  - This disables gyroscopic torque, which is acceptable but should be intentional. No deduction.

- **`emitter.SetParticlePositioner` usage:**  
  - The code uses `chrono.ChRandomParticlePositionOnGeometry()` with `chrono.ChBox(50, 50, 50)` and default `ChFramed()`. This is acceptable but the box is not added to the system or visualized, which is fine for emitter positioning. No deduction.
