### 2. Correctness (30 points)

- **Use of `SetSectionRotation`:** The code uses `msection.SetSectionRotation(45*chrono.CH_RAD_TO_DEG)`. According to PyChrono conventions, `CH_RAD_TO_DEG` is a factor to convert radians to degrees (≈57.2958). The argument 45 is presumably degrees, but multiplying by `CH_RAD_TO_DEG` converts 45 radians to degrees, which is incorrect. The correct usage would be either:

  - Provide the angle in radians and multiply by `CH_DEG_TO_RAD` (degrees to radians), or

  - Provide the angle in radians directly.

  This is a significant API misuse that could lead to incorrect beam section rotation.
