- **Joint Initialization:**

  - Similarly, `bearing` is a `ChLinkMateGeneric` initialized with `(builder.GetLastBeamNodes().back(), truss, ...)`. Again, the first argument is a node, not a body. This is incorrect usage and will cause errors or undefined behavior.

**Deductions:**

- Incorrect joint initialization with nodes instead of bodies (3 instances): **-15 points** (major error affecting simulation correctness)
