  The code uses `gear1.AddAsset(mat)` and `gear2.AddAsset(mat)`.  
  According to the API, materials are assigned during body creation or via collision models, not via `AddAsset()`.

  **Deduct 15 points for incorrect material assignment.**

  `ChLinkMateGeneric` is a mate (constraint) link, not a contact link.  
  Contact is handled automatically by the system when collision is enabled and materials are assigned properly.
