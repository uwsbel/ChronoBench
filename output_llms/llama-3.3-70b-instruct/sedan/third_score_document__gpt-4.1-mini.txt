  `chrono.ChContactMaterialNSC()` is used for the terrain patch, with friction and restitution set. This is correct.

- **PID Derivative Calculation:**  
  The derivative term is computed as `(pid_error - pid_derivative) / step_size`. This is incorrect: the derivative should be the difference between the current error and the previous error, not the previous derivative. This will cause incorrect derivative behavior and potentially unstable control.  
  **Deduction:** 10 points (logical error in PID derivative calculation).

  The throttle input is computed but never clamped to [0,1] or any valid range. This could cause unrealistic inputs.

  The driver is synchronized before inputs are fetched, which is correct.
