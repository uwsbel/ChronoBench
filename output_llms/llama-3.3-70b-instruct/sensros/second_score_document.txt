### 2. Correctness (30 points)

- **Use of `SetMass(0)` on fixed bodies:**  
  Setting mass to zero on fixed bodies is redundant but not incorrect. No deduction.
- **`SetAngVelParent` on ground body:**  
  The ground body is fixed (`SetFixed(True)`), but angular velocity is set via `SetAngVelParent`.  
  According to PyChrono principles, fixed bodies do not move, so setting angular velocity on a fixed body has no effect.  
  This is a logical error: the sensors attached to the ground body will not move as intended.  
  The correct approach would be to have a non-fixed body or a kinematic body to which angular velocity can be applied.  
  **Deduct 10 points** for logical error in setting angular velocity on a fixed body.
