Given the bodies are positioned at (-1,-3,0), (0,-3,0), and (1,-3,0), the spring attachment points are relative to each body’s frame.   - The `AddVisualShape()` method is used instead of the more common `AddVisualShape()` with a shape created via `ChBodyEasyBox` or `ChBodyEasySphere`. This is acceptable but less convenient.

**Deduction:**  
- The incorrect usage of `ChVisualShapeBox(1,1,1)` instead of `ChVisualShapeBox(chrono.ChVector3d(1,1,1))` is a significant API misuse that could cause runtime errors or incorrect visualization. Deduct **15 points** here.
