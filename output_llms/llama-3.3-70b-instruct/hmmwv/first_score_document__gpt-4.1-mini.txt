Also, the parts are manually added to the system via `sys.Add(hmmwv.GetPart(i))`. Usually, the vehicle class manages its own bodies and adding parts manually is not standard practice.  
  **Deduction: 10 points** (component present but lacks important details / incorrect configuration)

  **Deduction: 15 points** (incorrect body creation)

  **Deduction: 15 points** (missing essential visualization setup steps)
