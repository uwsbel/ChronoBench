  The vehicle is created as `veh.HMMWV()` and initialized with a coordinate system. Here, `vehicle.Initialize()` is called with a coordinate system, but no `SetInitPosition()` or `Initialize()` call is visible. This is a deviation from best practice and may cause incorrect initialization.  
  **Deduction: 10 points**

This suggests a missing or incorrect detail in terrain setup.
