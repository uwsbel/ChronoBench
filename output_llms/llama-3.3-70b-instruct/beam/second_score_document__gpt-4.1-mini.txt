### 2. Correctness (30 points)

- **Use of `ChBeamSectionEulerAdvanced`:** Correctly used with rectangular section and material properties set.  
- **Section Rotation:** The line `msection.SetSectionRotation(45 * chrono.CH_RAD_TO_DEG)` is suspicious. According to typical usage, `SetSectionRotation()` expects radians, but `chrono.CH_RAD_TO_DEG` is a constant to convert radians to degrees (≈57.2958). Multiplying 45 by this constant converts 45 radians to degrees, which is incorrect. It should be either `45 * chrono.CH_DEG_TO_RAD` (to convert degrees to radians) or just `chrono.CH_C_DEG_TO_RAD` if available. This is a significant API misuse that will cause incorrect rotation of the beam section.
