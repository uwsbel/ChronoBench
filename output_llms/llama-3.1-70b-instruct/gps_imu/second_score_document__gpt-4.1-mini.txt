### 2. Correctness (30 points)

- **API usage correctness:**  
  - The vehicle is created and initialized correctly.  
  - Visualization system usage is consistent with the vehicle module.  
  - Terrain patch is created with correct material and texture.  
  - Driver inputs are manually overridden in the loop, which is acceptable for a demo.  
  - Sensor creation and filter pushing are correct.
