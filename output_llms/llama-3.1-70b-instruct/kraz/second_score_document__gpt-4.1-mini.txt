### 2. Correctness (30 points)

- **API Usage:**  
  - The vehicle is created and initialized correctly.  
  - The terrain patch is created with correct material and size.  
  - Visualization system is properly initialized and attached to the vehicle.  
  - Driver inputs are synchronized and updated correctly.

  - The use of `driver.GetInputs()` and then modifying `driver_inputs.m_steering` directly is questionable. According to best practices, driver inputs should be set via driver methods or a proper interface, not by directly modifying the returned inputs object. This could cause unexpected behavior or be ignored by the driver.

The `Kraz` vehicle does not have a documented `GetTractor()` method in the API. This likely causes an error or unexpected behavior. - Missing linking of driver to vehicle (logical error).
